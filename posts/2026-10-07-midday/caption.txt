🦏 745 white rhinos have been moved from a breeding farm into 19 protected areas across Africa + 3 more wins you missed 👇

1. 🦏 745 rhinos moved from a farm into 19 wild parks
African Parks' Rhino Rewild has moved 745 southern white rhinos into 19 protected areas in 4 countries in 3 years. 89 calves have been born, though 61 rhinos died along the way.
🔗 Africa Geographic: https://africageographic.com/stories/rhino-rewild-three-years-745-rhinos-trials-and-successes/

2. 🚗 Gas-only cars dip below half of new car sales, a 1st
Gasoline-only cars made up 49% of global new car sales in early 2026, down from 73% in 2021. Electric cars took 17% and hybrids 18%.
🔗 Electrek: https://electrek.co/2026/10/05/gas-cars-fall-below-50-percent-global-new-car-sales/

3. 📚 A refugee teacher started a school in a camp with none
Isa fled war in Sudan and found no classrooms at a South Sudan settlement. He opened a school called "the future". Today more than 442 refugee children study across the camp's schools.
🔗 World Vision Canada: https://www.worldvision.ca/en/stories/against-the-odds-isas-mission-to-protect-education

4. 🧊 A telescope made of Antarctic ice wins the physics Nobel
Francis Halzen won for leading IceCube, which uses thousands of light sensors frozen into a cubic kilometre of ice to catch 'ghost particles' from distant galaxies.
🔗 BBC News: https://www.bbc.com/news/articles/cq203mymlvkeo

✨ Fun fact: Halzen first pitched catching neutrinos in South Pole ice in 1988.
