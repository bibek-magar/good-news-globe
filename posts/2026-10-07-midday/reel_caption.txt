🦏 745 rhinos moved from a farm into 19 wild parks

African Parks' Rhino Rewild has moved 745 southern white rhinos into 19 protected areas in 4 countries in 3 years. 89 calves have been born, though 61 rhinos died along the way.

Why it's cool: Spreading rhinos across many parks protects them from disease and poaching.
