🐢 A rare Blanding's turtle raised by schoolkids in Massachusetts 16 years ago just had her first hatchlings + 3 more wins you missed 👇

1. 🐢 A turtle raised by schoolkids 16 years ago just had babies
Zoo New England gives rare Blanding's turtle hatchlings to classrooms for a year, so they grow too big for predators. Small Fry, raised at a Concord school 16 years ago, now has hatchlings of her own.
🔗 GBH News: https://www.wgbh.org/news/local/2026-10-06/endangered-turtles-get-a-boost-with-the-help-of-massachusetts-school-kids

2. 🦵 A drug regrew worn knee cartilage in old mice
Stanford scientists blocked an ageing protein called 15-PGDH. Old mice regrew smooth knee cartilage, and human cartilage from knee replacements began making new tissue too. Human trials are still needed.
🔗 ScienceDaily: https://www.sciencedaily.com/releases/2026/10/261005011249.htm

3. 🏺 15 clay tablets reveal farm life 4,000+ years ago in Iraq
Researchers studied 15 cuneiform tablets in the Iraq Museum, dated 2112 to 2004 BCE. Most come from Umma and record land, irrigation, grain and workers.
🔗 Archaeology Magazine: https://archaeologymag.com/2026/10/ur-iii-tablets-reveal-royal-estates-in-mesopotamia/

4. 🔋 Australia's biggest coal plant site may get its biggest battery
Origin Energy wants to double the battery being built next to the Eraring coal station to 1,200 MW and 8,600 MWh. That would be Australia's largest by far.
🔗 RenewEconomy: https://reneweconomy.com.au/origin-wants-to-supersize-the-eraring-battery-to-make-it-easily-the-biggest-in-the-country/

✨ Fun fact: Blanding's turtle hatchlings are so small that predators as little as chipmunks can eat them.
