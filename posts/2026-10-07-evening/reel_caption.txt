🐢 A turtle raised by schoolkids 16 years ago just had babies

Zoo New England gives rare Blanding's turtle hatchlings to classrooms for a year, so they grow too big for predators. Small Fry, raised at a Concord school 16 years ago, now has hatchlings of her own.

Why it's cool: Kids helped save a turtle, and now she's helping her species recover.
