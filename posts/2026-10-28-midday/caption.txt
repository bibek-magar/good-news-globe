🐻 Two brown bears kept in tiny cages in Russia now explore an Irish forest, after a 5-year rescue by 100+ people + 3 more wins 👇

1. 🐻 2 brown bears from tiny Russian cages now live in a forest
Bu was a circus bear and Tyapa lived in a cage too low to stand in. After 5 years of planning, rescuers flew them from Moscow via Abu Dhabi to Wild Ireland's forest sanctuary.
🔗 People: https://www.yahoo.com/entertainment/articles/2-captive-brown-bears-rescued-200739680.html

2. 🐦 A 121-million-year-old toothed bird flew with no tail fan
Two finely preserved Longipteryx fossils show fluffy bodies but only short tufts at the rear. None of the 19 known specimens has tail feathers, unlike any bird alive today.
🔗 Phys.org: https://phys.org/news/2026-10-million-year-fossils-longipteryx-tail.html

3. ⭕ Earthquake sensors finally showed how a hula hoop stays up
Berkeley engineers strapped building-test sensors to a hoop, leg and foot. Their data overturned 20 years of thinking: the hoop's spin and its wobble are driven together, thighs to feet.
🔗 Phys.org: https://phys.org/news/2026-10-decoding-loopy-physics-hula-hooping.html

4. 🐆 A new Amazon reserve protects jaguars and river dolphins
Bolivia's new Agua Dulce reserve covers 715,000 acres along the Iténez River, home to jaguars, giant otters and 260 fish species. It links protected lands in Bolivia and Brazil.
🔗 Good News Network: https://www.goodnewsnetwork.org/ngo-celebrates-70-million-amazon-acres-conserved-after-protecting-are-called-a-link-of-life/

✨ Fun fact: The Iténez River runs about 780 miles, and most of it forms the border between Bolivia and Brazil.
