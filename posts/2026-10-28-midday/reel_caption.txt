🐻 2 brown bears from tiny Russian cages now live in a forest

Bu was a circus bear and Tyapa lived in a cage too low to stand in. After 5 years of planning, rescuers flew them from Moscow via Abu Dhabi to Wild Ireland's forest sanctuary.

Why it's cool: War closed one route and airspace closures another, but more than 100 people refused to give up.
