🚶 After 28 years and 30,000+ miles on foot from Chile, Karl Bushby is back in Britain, almost home + 3 more wins 👇

1. 🚶 After 28 years walking home, Karl Bushby is back in Britain
He left Chile in 1998 vowing never to use transport. He crossed the frozen Bering Strait on foot, swam the Caspian Sea and the English Channel, and now has about 250 miles left to Hull.
🔗 The Guardian: https://www.theguardian.com/lifeandstyle/2026/oct/13/karl-bushby-incredible-28-year-walk-home-goliath-expedition-hull

2. 🧬 Ancient human DNA was found on cave walls for the 1st time
Testing 24 rock art panels in 11 caves, scientists found ancient human DNA in 5 wall samples. At Portugal's Escoural cave, sealed until 1963, it is at least 4,000 to 5,000 years old.
🔗 Discover Magazine: https://www.discovermagazine.com/ancient-human-dna-on-cave-walls-could-reveal-who-touched-these-sites-and-their-art-49289

3. 🎤 180,000 fans signed up to volunteer for concert tickets
Harry Styles fans can earn tickets by volunteering through EARNT. In under 48 hours, 180,000 signed up, and in New York they've served food, helped seniors and planted trees.
🔗 PEOPLE: https://people.com/harry-styles-fans-are-turning-volunteering-into-concert-memories-it-felt-so-much-more-meaningful-exclusive-12164887

4. 🐨 A 178,000-hectare park for 12,000 koalas heads into law
NSW has introduced the bill to create the Great Koala National Park, after 10+ years of campaigning. It protects habitat for about 12,000 koalas, 36,000 gliders and 100+ threatened species.
🔗 Nature Conservation Council of NSW: https://www.nature.org.au/great_koala_national_park_legislation_historic_victory_for_nature_nsw
