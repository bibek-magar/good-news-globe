🐢 A survey counted about 180,000 giant tortoises on Aldabra in the Seychelles, the highest number ever recorded + 3 more wins 👇

1. 🐢 A record 180,000 giant tortoises roam one Seychelles atoll
A survey led by the Seychelles Islands Foundation and the University of Exeter counted about 180,000 Aldabra giant tortoises, the highest number ever recorded for the once-endangered species.
🔗 The Guardian: https://www.theguardian.com/environment/2026/oct/08/researchers-find-180000-giant-tortoises-living-on-island-in-seychelles

2. 📡 Webb traced the most distant fast radio burst yet
The MeerKAT telescope caught a millisecond radio flash, and Webb found its home: a tiny dwarf galaxy seen just 3 billion years after the Big Bang. Most such bursts come from far later.
🔗 NASA: https://science.nasa.gov/missions/webb/webb-measures-distance-to-farthest-fast-radio-burst-suggesting-origin/

3. 🐕 A Porto hospital lets patients' own pets visit them on the ward
Since July, São João hospital has let vaccinated, well-behaved pets visit long-stay patients on approval from their care team. It reports no infections so far.
🔗 Euronews: https://www.euronews.com/2026/10/08/portuguese-hospital-lets-pets-visit-owners-on-wards-a-new-model

4. 🏠 Hundreds of volunteers rebuilt a deaf family's ruined home
Ahmed and Rifat Ali lost £72,000 on an unsafe extension. Tradespeople and neighbours rebuilt the house for TV's DIY SOS, adding visual fire alarms and colour-changing alert lights.
🔗 BBC News: https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/cm369pglxd98o

✨ Fun fact: Aldabra giant tortoises are among the largest tortoises in the world.
