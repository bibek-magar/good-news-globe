🐢 A record 180,000 giant tortoises roam one Seychelles atoll

A survey led by the Seychelles Islands Foundation and the University of Exeter counted about 180,000 Aldabra giant tortoises, the highest number ever recorded for the once-endangered species.

Why it's cool: Decades of protection brought this giant back from the brink.
