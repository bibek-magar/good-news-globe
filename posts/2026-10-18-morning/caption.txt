✈️ At 99, a retired nurse rode strapped on top of a flying plane and became the world's oldest wing-walker + 3 more wins 👇

1. ✈️ At 99, she became the world's oldest wing-walker
Retired nurse Helga Duffy was strapped to the top of a small plane and flew for over 5 minutes, about 1,000 m up. Guinness confirmed the record, 3 months after a 97-year-old set it.
🔗 Good News Network (@goodnewsnetwork): https://www.goodnewsnetwork.org/99-yo-woman-snags-worlds-oldest-wing-walker-title/

2. 📲 A 17-year-old built an app that works when phone networks die
After the 2023 earthquakes cut phone service, Emirhan Duman built Crisis Connect: phones pass messages to each other over Bluetooth, hopping from device to device. It's free and open source.
🔗 Good News Network (@goodnewsnetwork): https://www.goodnewsnetwork.org/teens-disaster-messaging-app-can-hop-across-bluetooth-when-cellular-networks-collapse/

3. 💍 An orphaned bride asked for 2 chickens. A whole country came
A week before her wedding, Shahrazad said all she needed was 2 chickens for the meal. Algerians sent furniture, food and a gold jewellery set, and one donor pledged a monthly allowance.
🔗 Upworthy (@upworthy): https://scoop.upworthy.com/orphaned-bride-asked-for-2-chickens-for-her-wedding-strangers-arrived-with-gold-furniture-and-more

4. 🦬 A prairie herd passed 1,000 free-roaming bison for the 1st time
American Prairie's annual calf count put its herd at 1,016, the 1st time it has topped 1,000. The group hopes to reach 5,000 bison as it stitches together a giant reserve in Montana.
🔗 Good News Network (@goodnewsnetwork): https://www.goodnewsnetwork.org/largest-private-conservation-project-in-the-us-hits-major-milestone-as-free-roaming-bison-pass-1000/
