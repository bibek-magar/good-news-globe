✈️ At 99, she became the world's oldest wing-walker

Retired nurse Helga Duffy was strapped to the top of a small plane and flew for over 5 minutes, about 1,000 m up. Guinness confirmed the record, 3 months after a 97-year-old set it.

Why it's cool: Her niece saw the last record on the news and thought: Helga could do that.
