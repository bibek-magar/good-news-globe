🚀 Japan just launched MMX to land on Mars's moon Phobos and bring 10 g of its dust home to Earth + 3 more wins 👇

1. 🚀 Japan launched a mission to bring home dust from a Mars moon
JAXA's MMX lifted off on an H3 rocket and is now heading for Mars, arriving in August 2027. It plans 2 landings on the moon Phobos and a capsule landing in Australia in 2031.
🔗 Space.com: https://www.space.com/space-exploration/launches-spacecraft/japan-launches-historic-mmx-sample-return-mission-to-mars-moon-phobos

2. 🖼️ Copying a 700-year-old mural revealed the Buddha's head was off
Chinese artists spent 3 years copying the Met's 50-foot Medicine Buddha mural crack by crack. They found museum mounters had placed the Buddha's head 2 cm out of line.
🔗 Good News Network (@goodnewsnetwork): https://www.goodnewsnetwork.org/unprecedented-collab-between-china-and-the-met-uncovers-secrets-of-chinese-mural-during-exhaustive-reproduction/

3. 🐟 An endangered Australian fish was bred in captivity for the 1st time
Scientists in New South Wales bred the threatened Australian grayling in a hatchery, with fertility over 85%. The fish was hit hard by drought, the 2019-20 bushfires and floods.
🔗 Inside State Government: https://www.insidestategovernment.com.au/nsw-leads-world-first-breakthrough-in-endangered-fish-conservation/

4. 🦋 Mozambique protected its 'butterfly forest' on Mount Mabu
A government decree set aside 23,000 acres of Mount Mabu as a community conservation area. Its rainforest holds over 200 butterfly species and creatures found nowhere else.
🔗 Good News Network (@goodnewsnetwork): https://www.goodnewsnetwork.org/mozambique-protects-the-butterfly-forest-of-mount-mabu-home-to-200-species/
