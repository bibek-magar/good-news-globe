🐄 Painted eyes on cows' bums are keeping lions away

Farmer Edson Mabika used to lose 4 or 5 animals a year. Since painting eyes on his herd's rumps, he's lost just 2. In a Botswana study, lions and leopards killed none of 683 eye-painted cows.

Why it's cool: Ambush hunters hate being spotted, so fake eyes save both cattle and lions.
