1. 🐟 A fish with a see-through head was filmed in 4K
MBARI's robot sub met a barreleye fish about 600 m down, 90 km off California. It is only the 11th sighting in the institute's 40 years of deep-sea exploring.
🔗 Oceanographic: https://oceanographicmagazine.com/news/rare-footage-of-barreleye-fish-with-see-through-head/

2. 🌕 Tiny crystals from Artemis I mapped radiation near the Moon
Inside 2 human-shaped dummies on NASA's Orion, crystals tracked cosmic rays. Polish scientists found Moon radiation is higher than in Earth orbit, but by less than expected.
🔗 ScienceDaily: https://www.sciencedaily.com/releases/2026/10/261027063659.htm

3. ☀️ Scientists found why one crystal makes ammonia from sunlight
Teams in Vienna showed that 'frustrated' magnetism in triangles of 3 iron atoms lets a crystal turn nitrogen into ammonia with light. Swap in chromium and it stops.
🔗 Phys.org: https://phys.org/news/2026-10-atomic-magnetism-reveals-iron-chromium.html

4. 🦁 Farmers turned rangers keep Namibia's desert lions safe
Across 11 conservancies, Lion Rangers use smartphones, camera traps and GPS collars to warn farmers when lions come near, mixing tech with traditional tracking.
🔗 Positive News: https://www.positive.news/society/smartphones-ancient-tracking-skills-and-a-future-for-desert-lions/

✨ Fun fact: Namibia's desert lions grew from about 20 adults in 1997 to around 180 by 2015.
