🐱 One of the rarest cats on Earth shows up on a camera trap

A remote camera near La Payunia Reserve caught the elusive Andean cat, the 'ghost of the Andes', crossing rocky slopes. It's the 3rd sighting since 2024 on land being restored for wildlife.

Why it's cool: It's a sign that years of habitat restoration are working for one of the most endangered cats.
