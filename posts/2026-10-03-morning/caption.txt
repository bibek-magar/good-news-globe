🐱 One of the world's rarest wild cats, the Andean cat, was caught on a camera trap in Argentina + 3 more wins 👇

1. 🐱 One of the rarest cats on Earth shows up on a camera trap
A remote camera near La Payunia Reserve caught the elusive Andean cat, the 'ghost of the Andes', crossing rocky slopes. It's the 3rd sighting since 2024 on land being restored for wildlife.
🔗 PetaPixel (@petapixel): https://petapixel.com/2026/09/30/camera-trap-captures-rare-footage-of-endangered-andean-cat-living-in-remote-mountains/

2. 🪸 Coral 'probiotics' stopped bleaching during a heat wave
Scientists dosed Red Sea corals with friendly bacteria from healthy corals during a heat wave, diving 8 times in 15 days. Treated corals kept their color while the water hit 32°C.
🔗 Mongabay (@mongabay): https://news.mongabay.com/2026/10/probiotics-prevented-coral-bleaching-during-a-red-sea-heat-wave-study

3. 🌬️ A wind turbine taller than the Eiffel Tower just went up
Built near Berlin, the prototype's hub sits 300 m up and its blades reach 365 m. Its developer says winds that high could make more than twice the power of today's turbines.
🔗 ABC News (AP): https://abcnews.com/International/wireStory/wind-turbine-taller-eiffel-tower-aims-boost-german-136916621

4. 🏺 A 2,100-year-old wreck is full of luxury Roman-era dishes
Off Türkiye's coast, divers working 42-55 m deep are raising thousands of plates, bowls and trays in 122 forms. The ship likely sailed from Rhosus toward Italy before a storm sank it.
🔗 HeritageDaily: https://www.heritagedaily.com/2026/10/2100-year-old-luxury-tableware-discovered-in-ancient-shipwreck-off-turkiye/159487
