🥝 For the 1st time in 150 years, wild kiwi birds live in the hills around Wellington, New Zealand + 3 more wins you missed 👇

1. 🥝 Kiwi live wild around Wellington for the 1st time in 150 years
The Capital Kiwi Project, led with iwi and local landowners, moved 250+ kiwi back after trapping stoats. With chicks, there are now an estimated 300+ birds.
🔗 Mongabay: https://news.mongabay.com/podcast/2026/09/iwi-and-community-led-effort-return-kiwi-to-wellington-for-first-time-in-over-a-century/

2. 🛗 A 288 m cliff elevator cuts kids' school trip to 30 minutes
Children aged 6 to 15 used to spend about 6 hours a day walking to a mountaintop school and back. A glass elevator up the canyon wall now gets them there in about half an hour.
🔗 New Atlas: https://newatlas.com/engineering/watch-epic-cliff-elevator-china/

3. 🍋 A 6-year-old's $768 lemonade stand grew into $5M+ of help
Payton Obert set up a stand in 2018 to buy toys for kids in hospital, after a teddy bear comforted her there. Her nonprofit has since raised over $5 million in cash and goods.
🔗 Times of India: https://timesofindia.indiatimes.com/world/us/at-age-6-ohio-girl-payton-obert-raised-768-at-one-lemonade-stand-to-buy-toys-for-hospital-patients-eight-years-later-her-nonprofit-had-raised-more-than-5-million-in-money-and-donated-goods/articleshow/134657030.cms

4. 🏞️ Germany turned 23 old coal mines into a lake district
Flooded lignite pits in Lusatia now form 23 lakes with about 14,000 hectares of water. In 2026, five of them were linked into one connected waterway for boats.
🔗 Times of India: https://timesofindia.indiatimes.com/world/europe/germany-is-turning-abandoned-coal-mines-into-23-lakes-the-former-mining-landscape-is-becoming-europes-largest-artificial-wetland/articleshow/134725669.cms
