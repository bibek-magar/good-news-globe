🦧 8 orangutans rescued from Borneo's wildfires, including 3 mums with babies, are back in the forest + 3 more wins 👇

1. 🦧 8 orangutans saved from Borneo's fires are back in the wild
Three mother-and-baby pairs and two teen males, rescued from burning plantations, were moved to the 23,000-hectare Batu Nyambu Protected Forest. Rescuers have saved 46 orangutans from fires since August.
🔗 New Age (AFP): https://www.newagebd.net/post/asia/317496/indonesia-releases-eight-orangutans-rescued-from-fires-to-protected-forest

2. 💛 A Year 7 pupil spent her pocket money on a hurt stranger
When an elderly cyclist fell, Hannah Annakin stayed by his side until help came. Learning he hadn't eaten, she bought him food with her own pocket money. A paramedic asked her school to honour her.
🔗 Telegraph & Argus: https://www.thetelegraphandargus.co.uk/news/26618591.batley-schoolgirl-commended-yorkshire-ambulance-service/

3. 🪷 A selfie with a giant waterlily revealed a new species
A US plant grower posted a photo of himself beside a giant waterlily in Colombia. A Kew botanist spotted it was unusual, and an expedition found seven plants: Victoria colombiana.
🔗 Gizmodo: https://gizmodo.com/someone-posted-an-instagram-selfie-scientists-spotted-a-giant-new-species-in-the-background-2000823673

4. 🏹 Norway's ice has given up 5,000+ ancient artefacts
In 20 years at 73 sites, the Secrets of the Ice team has found bows, arrows, shoes and clothing. This year's finds include a slate arrowhead between 5,000 and 6,000 years old.
🔗 GB News: https://gbnews.com/science/archeology-breakthrough-worlds-biggest-glacial-expedition-stone-age-discovery
