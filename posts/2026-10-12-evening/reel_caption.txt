🦧 8 orangutans saved from Borneo's fires are back in the wild

Three mother-and-baby pairs and two teen males, rescued from burning plantations, were moved to the 23,000-hectare Batu Nyambu Protected Forest. Rescuers have saved 46 orangutans from fires since August.

Why it's cool: Every one counts: about 104,000 orangutans are left, down from 230,000 a century ago.
