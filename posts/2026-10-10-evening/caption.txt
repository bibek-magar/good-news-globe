🏝️ Guano miners left Lady Elliot Island on the Great Barrier Reef bare for 100 years. One family replanted it, and the seabirds are back + 3 more wins 👇

1. 🏝️ A family turned a mined-out reef island back into a bird haven
Guano mining in the 1860s left Lady Elliot Island bare for a century. From the 1960s the Adams family began replanting; now over 20,000 plants cover about 70% of it and seabirds nest again.
🔗 ABC News: https://www.abc.net.au/news/2026-10-10/guano-mine-rehabilitation-on-lady-elliot-island-birds-return/107138364

2. 🍦 A super-sweet fruit protein halved blood sugar spikes
In a double-blind trial, ice cream sweetened with brazzein, a protein first found in a West African fruit, caused blood sugar rises about half as big as sugar ice cream did.
🔗 ScienceAlert: https://www.sciencealert.com/a-protein-2000-times-sweeter-than-sugar-halved-ice-creams-blood-sugar-response

3. 🏺 A 6,500-year-old clay figurine turns up in Transylvania
At the Dâmbul Cetății site, archaeologists found a Copper Age female figurine of the Cucuteni culture. It cracked exactly where its maker joined two pieces of clay, revealing how it was made.
🔗 Ancientist: https://ancientist.com/a-6500-year-old-female-figurine-has-been-discovered-in-transylvania/

4. 🐋 Rescuers helped stranded young humpbacks back to the sea
Two juvenile humpback whales got stuck on sandbars off Fire Island. With rising tides and help from crews including the US Coast Guard, both made it back to open water.
🔗 ABC13: https://abc13.com/story/us-coast-guard-helps-rescue-juvenile-humpback-whale-stuck-sandbar-off-new-yorks-fire-island/19921301/

✨ Fun fact: Brazzein keeps its sweetness even when heated to 80 °C.
