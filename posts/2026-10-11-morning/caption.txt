🦪 85% of the world's oyster reefs are gone, but restoring them could deliver $12 trillion in benefits + 3 more wins 👇

1. 🦪 Restoring oyster reefs could deliver $12 trillion in benefits
85% of oyster reefs vanished in a century. A Tsinghua University study estimates restored reefs in 10 countries would give $12 trillion in clean water, carbon storage and coast protection.
🔗 SciTechDaily: https://scitechdaily.com/scientists-reveal-a-staggering-12-trillion-potential-hidden-in-the-worlds-oyster-reefs/

2. 🌊 Aarhus now heats 1,900 homes with water from its bay
Six seawater heat pumps on the harbour front draw up to 1.2 million litres an hour to heat about 1,900 homes. Eight more are due from 2028.
🔗 ECOnews: https://www.ecoticias.com/en/aarhus-in-denmark-switched-on-six-seawater-heat-pumps-that-pull-up-to-317000-gallons-of-bay-water-an-hour-to-warm-about-1900-homes-and-eight-more-are-coming-to-help-replace-wood-pellets/51819/

3. 🗣️ Yarning circles help preschoolers learn an Aboriginal language
Curtin researchers mixed traditional 'yarning' storytelling with songs and play to teach Noongar to preschoolers, who joined in more confidently.
🔗 Curtin University: https://www.curtin.edu.au/research/news/new-teaching-method-could-help-aboriginal-languages-survive-and-thrive?type=media

4. 👶 Two NYPD officers saved a choking baby, then met him again
Officer Julio Arias gave an unconscious 11-month-old back blows until he cried and breathed again. The boy later visited the precinct to say thanks.
🔗 Officer.com: https://www.officer.com/on-the-street/body-cameras/news/55409444/nypd-police-officers-reunite-with-baby-saved-during-dramatic-choking-emergency
