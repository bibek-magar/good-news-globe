🦪 Restoring oyster reefs could deliver $12 trillion in benefits

85% of oyster reefs vanished in a century. A Tsinghua University study estimates restored reefs in 10 countries would give $12 trillion in clean water, carbon storage and coast protection.

Why it's cool: Oysters filter water and shield coasts, and there's room to bring them back.
