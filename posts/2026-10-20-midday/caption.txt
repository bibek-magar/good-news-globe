🍄 A giant fungus up to 50 cm tall and 2.2 kg was found in China, and it's so unique it got a brand-new family + 3 more wins 👇

1. 🍄 A 2.2 kg fungus in China was so unique it got its own family
Dianjunus rex pushes pale yellow columns up through the forest floor, reaching about 50 cm with its buried black stalk. DNA showed it belongs to a brand-new fungal family.
🔗 Earth.com (@earthdotcom): https://www.earth.com/plants/scientists-discover-a-massive-fungus-with-a-towering-body-that-belongs-to-a-whole-new-family-born-just-after-the-dinosaurs-went-extinct/

2. 😴 Removing brain immune cells gave Alzheimer's mice 2+ hrs of sleep
University of Kentucky scientists found overactive immune cells, not plaques, drove sleep loss. Removing most of them gave the mice over 2 extra hours of deep sleep a day.
🔗 ScienceDaily: https://www.sciencedaily.com/releases/2026/10/261018084421.htm

3. 🌟 A dying star that came back to life is heating up again
Sakurai's Object reignited in the 1990s and hid behind dust. Using the Very Large Telescope, astronomers measured it again: about 30,500 K, its 1st temperature reading since 1998.
🔗 Earth.com (@earthdotcom): https://www.earth.com/space/a-dying-star-has-come-back-to-life-and-scientists-are-watching/

4. ⚓ A ship wrecked in 1873 may have been found near Patagonia
On a remote island, archaeologists found a 19th-century ship's timbers and a castaway camp with bottles, buttons and tools. They likely belong to Luis Piedra Buena's stranded crew.
🔗 VICE (@vice): https://www.vice.com/en/article/archaeologists-uncovered-a-150-year-old-shipwreck-that-may-have-belonged-to-stranded-penguin-hunters/

✨ Fun fact: Only 2 'born-again' stars have been seen reigniting: Sakurai's Object and V605 Aquilae.
