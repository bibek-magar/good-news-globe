🐶 70+ dogs rescued from a farm months before Korea's ban

Humane World for Animals Korea took in the last 70+ dogs at a farm that once held up to 1,000. They go to shelters in the US and Canada to be adopted. A full ban on dog meat starts in 2027.

Why it's cool: The group calls it likely its last big rescue as the industry ends.
