🐶 More than 70 dogs were rescued from a dog meat farm in South Korea and are flying to new families + 3 more wins you missed 👇

1. 🐶 70+ dogs rescued from a farm months before Korea's ban
Humane World for Animals Korea took in the last 70+ dogs at a farm that once held up to 1,000. They go to shelters in the US and Canada to be adopted. A full ban on dog meat starts in 2027.
🔗 The Korea Herald: https://biz.heraldcorp.com/article/10890952

2. 👁️ Gene therapy partly restored sight in blind patients
One eye injection made surviving retina cells sensitive to light, and special goggles feed them images. In a 10-person trial, 6 had meaningful gains, and some could find a door or touch a notebook.
🔗 The Guardian: https://www.theguardian.com/society/2026/oct/07/gene-therapy-can-partly-restore-sight-in-blind-people-researchers-reveal

3. 🏝️ River sand became 7 new wildlife islands on the Mississippi
Engineers used 500,000 cubic yards of sand dredged from the river channel to build 7 islands in McGregor Lake, and planted them to grow forest for eagles, herons and songbirds.
🔗 DredgeWire: https://dredgewire.com/corps-of-engineers-celebrates-mcgregor-lake-restoration-project-completion/

4. 🏺 A 2,100-year-old shipwreck held thousands of intact dishes
Divers off Turkey found a Rome-bound cargo ship carrying thousands of plates and bowls in 122 styles, packed in clay inside crates. They likely came from Rhosus, a town Cicero ordered dishes from.
🔗 Times of India: https://timesofindia.indiatimes.com/science/archaeology/2100-year-old-shipwreck-filled-with-122-varieties-of-ancient-roman-tableware-found-by-archaeologists-in-turkey-was-mentioned-in-a-letter-by-roman-statesman-cicero/articleshow/134584079.cms

✨ Fun fact: Cicero wrote about ordering dishes from Rhosus in a letter around 50 BCE.
