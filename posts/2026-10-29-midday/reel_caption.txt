🦎 Europe's fire salamanders secretly glow under UV light

Under ultraviolet light, the black-and-yellow amphibians glow green and cyan, mostly from skin glands. Their toxic secretions kept glowing for more than 24 hours.

Why it's cool: Decades of studying their skin chemistry never spotted it, and the glowing compound is still unknown.
