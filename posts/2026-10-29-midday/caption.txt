1. 🦎 Europe's fire salamanders secretly glow under UV light
Under ultraviolet light, the black-and-yellow amphibians glow green and cyan, mostly from skin glands. Their toxic secretions kept glowing for more than 24 hours.
🔗 ScienceDaily: https://www.sciencedaily.com/releases/2026/10/261027063707.htm

2. ☄️ 75 meteorite falls show space rocks melt, then shatter
Studying videos of 75 meteorite falls, SETI Institute and NASA scientists found 7 stages. Melting alone can strip up to 40% of a rock before air pressure breaks it apart.
🔗 ScienceDaily: https://www.sciencedaily.com/releases/2026/10/261027063710.htm

3. ⚛️ Physicists controlled the magnetic core of a single atom
A team at TU Delft used a modified scanning tunnelling microscope to flip the magnetism of one atom's nucleus with radio waves, something thought impossible to do directly.
🔗 Phys.org: https://phys.org/news/2026-10-magnetic-core-atom.html

4. 🗼 Scientists explained the 'phantom lighthouse' on the horizon
Visitors see a beam shining from the sea when the lighthouse is behind them. A study recreated it in virtual reality: in the dark, the brain misjudges how far and fast the beam moves.
🔗 Phys.org: https://phys.org/news/2026-10-phantom-lighthouse-australia-strangest-illusions.html

✨ Fun fact: The first Lady Elliot Island lighthouse was built in 1873.
