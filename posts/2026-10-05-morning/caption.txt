🐒 Brazil vaccinated black-faced lion tamarins against yellow fever for the 1st time. Fewer than 400 remain + 3 more wins 👇

1. 🐒 Rare tamarins get their first-ever yellow fever shots
Vets in Brazil's Atlantic Forest vaccinated 3 black-faced lion tamarins, a species with fewer than 400 left and none in zoos. All 3 went back to the trees and have shown no side effects.
🔗 Mongabay (@mongabay): https://news.mongabay.com/2026/10/a-first-in-brazil-black-faced-lion-tamarins-vaccinated/

2. 🏺 A landmine sweep turned up Korea's oldest verified gun
Found during a safety survey on Mount Geumseong, a 25 cm bronze hand cannon has been confirmed as the earliest authentic Korean firearm. Its inscription even names the craftsman, Suk.
🔗 HeritageDaily (@archaeologynews): https://www.heritagedaily.com/2026/10/bronze-hand-cannon-offers-rare-evidence-of-early-korean-firearms/159507

3. 💊 Vitamin B3 shows early promise against a deadly brain cancer
In a trial of 24 people with glioblastoma, adding high-dose niacin to standard care left 82% without tumour growth after 6 months, 28% better than past studies. Bigger tests are next.
🔗 ScienceDaily: https://www.sciencedaily.com/releases/2026/10/261001095423.htm

4. 🐚 A giant clam kept a 30-year climate diary in its shell
Counting nearly 10,900 daily lines and drilling 784 tiny samples, scientists rebuilt the Great Barrier Reef's climate month by month from 1785 to 1827. Summers then were up to 1.2°C cooler.
🔗 Phys.org: https://phys.org/news/2026-09-giant-clam-monthbymonth-diary-great.html

✨ Fun fact: Giant clams can grow 1.2 m wide, weigh over 200 kg and live for more than a century.
