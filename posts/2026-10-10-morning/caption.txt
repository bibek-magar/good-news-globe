🐒 8 rope bridges over an Amazon road gave monkeys and marmosets 15,000 safe crossings, with zero roadkill + 3 more wins 👇

1. 🐒 Rope bridges gave Amazon animals 15,000 safe road crossings
Eight canopy bridges strung over a road near Alta Floresta let spider monkeys, marmosets and capuchins cross 15,000 times in 15 months, with not a single roadkill recorded.
🔗 The Guardian: https://www.theguardian.com/environment/2026/oct/09/animals-crossing-simple-solution-roadkill-amazon-brazil-alternatives

2. 🏛️ A Roman temple to Mars turned up just under a forest floor
Beside the Kapersburg fort, archaeologists found a small temple with an inscription to Mars and a winged statuette of Victory.
🔗 Smithsonian: https://www.smithsonianmag.com/smart-news/just-inches-below-the-forest-floor-archaeologists-in-germany-unearthed-a-roman-temple-likely-dedicated-to-mars-the-god-of-war-180989650/

3. 🔋 A smarter battery design could last 20% longer
Chalmers researchers modelled an EV battery pack that bypasses worn-out cells. In one typical case it delayed a battery swap by about 14 months.
🔗 Chalmers University: https://www.chalmers.se/en/current/news/e2-new-battery-architecture-could-extend-ev-battery-life-and-lower-ownership-costs/

4. ☁️ Webb watched water clouds change on a world 7.5 light-years away
Webb tracked WISE 0855, the coldest known brown dwarf, for 11 hours. Its water clouds grew thicker and thinner: the first such weather confirmed beyond our solar system.
🔗 EurekAlert!: https://www.eurekalert.org/news-releases/1147098

✨ Fun fact: WISE 0855 is about 265 K, colder than Earth's average surface temperature.
