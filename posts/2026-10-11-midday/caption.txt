☀️🌽 Solar panels 5 m above a French corn field shaded the crop through a heatwave, and yields rose almost 20% + 3 more wins 👇

1. 🌽 Solar canopies boosted corn yields by almost 20% in a heatwave
In eastern France, where it topped 35°C for weeks, 5,500 solar panels raised 5 m over a maize field shaded the crop. Yields rose almost 20% versus open ground, while the panels made clean power.
🔗 Good Green News: https://goodgreen.news/a-solution-for-the-future-solar-canopies-produced-clean-energy-while-boosting-crop-yields-by-20-during-summer-heatwaves/

2. 🧬 A rapid gene test gives kids' cancer results in 2–3 days
At Addenbrooke's Hospital, fast whole-genome tests on 54 children returned results in 2–3 days instead of 4–6 weeks. Some children avoided surgery, and a national NHS trial of 200 kids has begun.
🔗 The Independent: https://www.independent.co.uk/news/uk/home-news/nhs-cancer-test-wgs-treatment-b3061971.html

3. 🐍 A new egg-eating snake species was found in Ethiopia
Scientists described the white-throated egg-eater, an ink-black, harmless snake from Ethiopia's Harenna Forest that eats only bird eggs, cracking them with special bones in its throat.
🔗 Herpetozoa (Pensoft): https://herpetozoa.pensoft.net/article/205043/

4. 🥬 Canadian students grow fresh salad indoors for school meals
In remote towns where lettuce arrives wilted, three schools use hydroponic towers. In Dinsmore, six towers gave 40 heads of lettuce in two months; in Eskasoni they feed 264+ students.
🔗 Happy Eco News: https://happyeconews.com/growing-without-soil-and-without-limits-how-canadian-schools-are-using-hydroponics-to-fight-food-insecurity/

✨ Fun fact: Female white-throated egg-eaters grow up to 92 cm long, males only up to 58 cm.
