🐬 6 baby dolphins were born on Cambodia's Mekong River

This calving season brought 6 Irrawaddy dolphin calves, taking the group on a 75-mile stretch of the Mekong to 118. It is the largest population of the species still living in a river.

Why it's cool: Since 2014, WWF has helped riverside families earn more beyond fishing, which threatens calves.
