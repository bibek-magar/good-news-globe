🌳 Australia just opened 3 new national parks, 250+ square miles of forest for gliders, owls and platypuses + 3 more wins 👇

1. 🌳 3 new national parks now protect gliders, owls and platypuses
More than 250 square miles of forest in central Victoria became Mount Buangor, Pyrenees and Wombat-Lerderderg national parks, the state's first big new parks in 15 years, after a campaign that began in 2010.
🔗 Good News Network: https://www.goodnewsnetwork.org/australia-announces-3-new-national-parks-to-protect-gliders-owls-and-other-victoria-wildlife/

2. 🏺 A 2,300-year-old shipwreck still holds its sailors' cups
Off the Croatian island of Vis, divers found part of the hull, coins and rare Greek drinking cups and plates. Some vessels bear scratched markings that may name the sailors who owned them.
🔗 PEOPLE: https://people.com/divers-investigate-2-300-year-old-shipwreck-and-find-ancient-sailors-belongings-still-preserved-12170496

3. 🥎 A softball player found her long-lost sister on the other team
Adopted as a baby, Samantha Rensvold was told a rival player looked just like her. The name on the roster matched her birth sister's, and both moms confirmed the same birth mother and hospital.
🔗 Good News Network: https://www.goodnewsnetwork.org/montana-high-school-softball-player-discovered-long-lost-sister-on-opposing-team/

4. 🍋 A girl's lemonade stand has raised $5 million for kids
At 8, Payton Obert sold lemonade to buy toys for kids in hospital, like the teddy that helped her as a baby. She raised $768. 8 years on, her stands have sent $5 million in toys, food and clothes to kids.
🔗 Good News Network: https://www.goodnewsnetwork.org/teens-wish-to-return-kindness-she-received-sees-her-lemonade-stand-become-5-million-charity-engine/
