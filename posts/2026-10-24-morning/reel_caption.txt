🌿 A no-strings cash trial cut food insecurity from 73% to 26%

For 2 years, 211 people in 3 Indigenous Amazon communities got about £2 a day to spend freely. Food insecurity dropped sharply and 90% took up greener farming practices.

Why it's cool: It was small, with no control group, but the charity behind it plans to expand.
