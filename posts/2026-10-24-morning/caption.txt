🌿 A no-strings cash trial in the Peruvian Amazon cut food insecurity from 73% to 26% + 3 more wins you missed 👇

1. 🌿 A no-strings cash trial cut food insecurity from 73% to 26%
For 2 years, 211 people in 3 Indigenous Amazon communities got about £2 a day to spend freely. Food insecurity dropped sharply and 90% took up greener farming practices.
🔗 Positive News: https://www.positive.news/society/good-news-stories-from-week-43-of-2026/

2. 🐦 Seabird poo helps reefs grow up to 2.5 times faster
On 28 tropical islands, reefs beside thriving seabird colonies grew up to 2.5x faster than those near rat-infested atolls, and made more sand to build the islands.
🔗 Positive News: https://www.positive.news/society/good-news-stories-from-week-43-of-2026/

3. 🌱 A plant-covered Bangladeshi rug factory won a top world prize
Draped in plants and gardens, Rangpur's Green Field factory cools itself naturally and gives workers views, a medical centre, daycare and a canteen. It won the RIBA International Prize.
🔗 Positive News: https://www.positive.news/society/good-news-stories-from-week-43-of-2026/

4. 🦭 An orphaned baby walrus found alone on a beach was saved
Annautaq, a 5-month-old calf, was hiding beside the remains of 2 adult walruses on a remote Alaska beach. Rescuers cared for him, then flew him 4,000 miles to join 3 walruses in Florida.
🔗 Good News Network (@goodnewsnetwork): https://www.goodnewsnetwork.org/rescuers-ensure-orphaned-baby-walrus-gets-a-second-chance-look/

✨ Fun fact: The walrus calf's name, Annautaq, means 'axe' in Iñupiaq, honouring his rescuer's father.
