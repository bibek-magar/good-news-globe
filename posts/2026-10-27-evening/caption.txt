🦕 A dinosaur dug up in a Surrey brick quarry in 2001 has just been named a brand-new species + 3 more wins you missed 👇

1. 🦕 A dinosaur found in 2001 is a brand-new species
Dug from a Surrey brick quarry and kept at London's Natural History Museum, Paludohadros proudlovii was a rhino-sized young plant-eater, about 5 m long and up to 2 tonnes.
🔗 Natural History Museum: https://www.nhm.ac.uk/press-office/press-releases/new-uk-dinosaur-named-after-devoted-dad-and-husband.html

2. 💎 Diamond flaws just proved an 80-year-old physics theory
Using tiny quantum sensors inside a lab-grown diamond, a NIMS team mapped the stress around a single atomic flaw, about 10 times more precisely than older methods. It matched the theory.
🔗 Phys.org: https://phys.org/news/2026-10-diamond-defects-year-theory-crystal.html

3. 🍄 Kew solved a 245-year mix-up over a famous medicinal mushroom
Since 1781, Europe's Ganoderma lucidum and East Asia's prized lingzhi, or reishi, were often treated as one. Old Kew specimens show they split at least 16 million years ago.
🔗 Phys.org: https://phys.org/news/2026-10-false-lingzhi-unmasked-scientists-year.html

4. 🐢 Fishers raised the alarm, and 5 sea turtles swam free
Five female green sea turtles got caught in fish corrals. Local fishers reported them, and rescuers tagged and released them unhurt; the largest shell was 78 cm long.
🔗 Inquirer: https://newsinfo.inquirer.net/2330314/5-female-green-sea-turtles-rescued-from-fish-corrals-in-aurora

✨ Fun fact: The new dinosaur's name, Paludohadros, comes from 'palus', Latin for swamp.
