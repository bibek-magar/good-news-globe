🚙 NASA's Perseverance rover has now driven 45 km on Mars, farther than any vehicle on another world + 3 more wins 👇

1. 🚙 Perseverance has now driven farther than any vehicle off Earth
NASA's rover passed Opportunity's 45.16 km on Mars, covering in 5.5 years what took its predecessor almost 15. It steered itself for more than 90% of the drive.
🔗 Earth.com (@earthdotcom): https://www.earth.com/space/nasas-perseverance-rover-has-now-driven-farther-than-any-vehicle-on-another-world/

2. 🐦 2 new contenders for the world's loudest bird
Biologists measured 123 bird species in the wild. The bare-throated bellbird and the red-legged seriema joined the white bellbird above 120 decibels, with peaks of about 127 to 131 dB.
🔗 Earth.com (@earthdotcom): https://www.earth.com/animals/what-is-the-worlds-loudest-bird-scientists-identify-two-new-contenders/

3. 🖤 Botanists named a black, horned 'devil flower' in Thailand
Thismia daemona makes no chlorophyll and lives off fungi in the soil, rising only to flower. Fewer than 50 plants are known, all on one hillside in Thong Pha Phum National Park.
🔗 Earth.com (@earthdotcom): https://www.earth.com/plants/devil-flower-new-species-thailand/

4. 📜 6 lost pages of a medieval book turned up in an English archive
Mislabelled papers in Brighton turned out to be missing pages of a 15th-century religious book now at Yale. Its owner, a Henry VIII official, kept it while he was meant to destroy such books.
🔗 Earth.com (@earthdotcom): https://www.earth.com/science/lost-pages-of-a-medieval-manuscript-reveal-a-secret-hidden-for-centuries/

✨ Fun fact: Before Perseverance, NASA's first Mars rover, Sojourner, drove only about 100 m in 1997.
