🚙 Perseverance has now driven farther than any vehicle off Earth

NASA's rover passed Opportunity's 45.16 km on Mars, covering in 5.5 years what took its predecessor almost 15. It steered itself for more than 90% of the drive.

Why it's cool: It has already beaten the Apollo 17 Moon buggy and the Soviet Lunokhod 2 rover too.
