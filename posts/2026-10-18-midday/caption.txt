🚴 A cyclist rode the old Pony Express mail route across America and beat the riders' 1861 record by nearly 20 hours + 3 more wins 👇

1. 🚴 A cyclist beat a 165-year-old Pony Express record
Lachlan Morton rode the 1,966-mile route from St. Joseph, Missouri, to Sacramento in 6 days, 21 hours and 15 minutes, swapping between 3 bikes through mud, sand, headwinds and the Sierra Nevada.
🔗 Velo: https://velo.outsideonline.com/news/cyclist-lachlan-morton-beats-pony-express-record-by-nearly-20-hours/

2. 🔭 AI helped find 70 new natural telescopes in space
An AI scanned a map of nearly 4 billion objects for gravitational lenses, where a galaxy's gravity magnifies one behind it. A telescope in Chile then confirmed 70 of 76 candidates.
🔗 NSF NOIRLab: https://noirlab.edu/public/blog/desi-legacy-imaging-surveys/

3. 🦆 India added 2 lakes and now protects 103 world-class wetlands
Sukhna Lake in Chandigarh, at the foot of the Himalayas, and Sauj Jheel in Uttar Pradesh joined the global Ramsar list of protected wetlands, taking India's total to 103.
🔗 Onmanorama (@onmanorama): https://onmanorama.com/upsc/current-affairs/2026/10/16/india-now-has-103-ramsar-sites.html

4. 💉 Egypt cut hepatitis C from 10% to under 0.4%, and kept it there
A national campaign screened 60 million+ people and treated 4.5 million+ with locally made medicine. Egypt is now the 1st country the WHO has confirmed kept its 'gold tier' status.
🔗 WHO EMRO: https://www.emro.who.int/media/news/egypt-is-the-first-country-globally-to-maintain-gold-tier-status-on-the-path-to-hepatitis-c-elimination.html

✨ Fun fact: The Pony Express ran for only 18 months before the telegraph made it obsolete in 1861.
