🚴 A cyclist beat a 165-year-old Pony Express record

Lachlan Morton rode the 1,966-mile route from St. Joseph, Missouri, to Sacramento in 6 days, 21 hours and 15 minutes, swapping between 3 bikes through mud, sand, headwinds and the Sierra Nevada.

Why it's cool: He wanted to feel what the young mail riders went through crossing the West in 1861.
