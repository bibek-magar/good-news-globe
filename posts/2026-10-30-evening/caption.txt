1. 🎋 Bamboo and oyster shells stopped a village's floods
In Pamarawan, the Philippines' first nature-based sea defence uses bamboo cribs plus oyster shells in coconut-husk nets to break waves. The village no longer floods.
🔗 Mongabay: https://news.mongabay.com/2026/10/philippine-village-uses-nature-to-defend-against-floods-with-bamboo-shells-and-husks/

2. 🌌 The universe's oldest light reveals spinning cosmic gas
Columbia astronomers averaged Big Bang afterglow data around 25 galaxy clusters and found a faint hot-cold pattern: evidence that gas inside the clusters is rotating.
🔗 Phys.org: https://phys.org/news/2026-10-cosmic-microwave-background-evidence-gas.html

3. ☀️ More bright daylight was linked to 16% less dementia
Wrist sensors tracked light for 87,577 adults over about 8 years. Those in brighter daytime light had a 16% lower dementia risk; under 42 minutes a day was a strong warning sign.
🔗 ScienceDaily: https://www.sciencedaily.com/releases/2026/10/261028232707.htm

4. 🐠 Fishers on Pemba asked for new marine parks, and got 2
After catches kept shrinking, more than 20 communities on Pemba's east coast worked with Tanzania's government and NGOs to create 2 marine protected areas they help manage.
🔗 Mongabay: https://news.mongabay.com/2026/10/pembas-new-marine-protected-areas-show-the-power-of-collaboration-to-meet-30x30-goal/

✨ Fun fact: The Pamarawan project cost about $2 million and was inspired by mangrove work in Indonesia.
