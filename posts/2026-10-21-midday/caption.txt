Archaeologists studied a stone ruin for 20 years without proof. Then a grave facing east, the Christian way, confirmed it: the first new Norse church found in Greenland in over 60 years.

3. 🕷️ 12 new species of 'living fossil' spiders were found in China
These trapdoor spiders still have 12 armour plates on their backs, a trait most spiders lost long ago. The finds lift their genus from 43 known species to 55.
🔗 Nature World News: https://www.natureworldnews.com/articles/73594/20261021/twelve-new-segmented-trapdoor-spiders-guizhou-push-ancient-genus-55-known-species.htm

4. 🐕 A rescue dog who sniffs out 'sea vomit' won Animal of the Year
Sgiath, a 6-year-old Labrador, swapped mountain rescue for the coast. She smells out an invasive sea squirt nicknamed 'sea vomit' and has found hundreds of colonies.
🔗 The Independent: https://www.independent.co.uk/bulletin/news/sea-vomit-dog-iwaf-award-b3067292.html

✨ Fun fact: When Sgiath finds sea vomit, she doesn't bark: she freezes and stares at it.
