🚀 ESA's Juice gains 3.5 km/s by swinging past Earth

Juice passed 8,640 km above Earth, and our planet's gravity bent its path by 20° and sped it up, using very little fuel. It's on its way to 35 flybys of Jupiter's icy moons.

Why it's cool: Saved fuel means more time studying moons that may hide oceans.
