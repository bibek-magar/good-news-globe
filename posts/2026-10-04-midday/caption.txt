🚀 ESA's Juice spacecraft swung past Earth and gained 3.5 km/s on its way to Jupiter, using almost no fuel + 3 more wins you missed 👇

1. 🚀 ESA's Juice gains 3.5 km/s by swinging past Earth
Juice passed 8,640 km above Earth, and our planet's gravity bent its path by 20° and sped it up, using very little fuel. It's on its way to 35 flybys of Jupiter's icy moons.
🔗 ScienceDaily (@europeanspaceagency): https://www.sciencedaily.com/releases/2026/10/261001214102.htm

2. 🪺 Melbourne puts out 100 'bricks' birds can build nests from
Made of bark, twigs, coconut fibre and moss, the bricks slowly break down so birds like fairy wrens can take natural material instead of plastic and cigarette butts.
🔗 Phys.org: https://phys.org/news/2026-10-natural-material-bricks-aim-urban.html

3. 🔥 Village women kept their forest fire-free 3 years running
In Marhmanley, Himalayan women led by council head Kamla Devi plan small controlled burns and rush to put out sparks with branch brooms, so the village has avoided major blazes.
🔗 Mongabay (@mongabay): https://news.mongabay.com/2026/09/sickles-sarees-and-smoke-women-on-the-frontlines-of-uttarakhand-forest-fires/

4. 🪵 Divers find 10,500-year-old timbers at a lake bottom
Under 20 m of water, divers recovered a 1.6 m post with chop marks and split boards up to 1.3 m long, left by Stone Age hunter-gatherers. Wood this old almost never survives.
🔗 HeritageDaily (@archaeologynews): https://www.heritagedaily.com/2026/10/10500-year-old-wooden-structures-discovered-beneath-german-lake/159500
