🦦 A river otter was caught on camera on California's San Mateo coast, the first seen there since the 1800s + 3 more wins you missed 👇

1. 🦦 A river otter is back on California's San Mateo coast
An amateur naturalist's trail cameras on a 5,100-acre preserve caught a river otter 3 times, the first documented on that coast since the 1800s fur trade wiped them out.
🔗 Santa Cruz Sentinel: https://santacruzsentinel.com/2026/10/24/river-otter-spotted-on-san-mateo-coast-is-believed-to-be-first-since-1800s

2. 🦖 A 150-million-year-old bird had dinosaur teeth and couldn't fly
The crow-sized Yuezhuornis had serrated teeth, a sickle-shaped toe claw and legs built for the ground. It's the earliest known bird lineage to lose the ability to fly.
🔗 ScienceDaily: https://www.sciencedaily.com/releases/2026/10/261023023710.htm

3. 🛰️ Satellites now tell rangers which wild toilets need a visit
New Zealand put 50+ satellite-linked sensors at 20+ remote huts and toilets, so crews only fly in when needed, cutting helicopter trips and about 30 tonnes of CO2.
🔗 Yahoo News: https://www.yahoo.com/news/science/articles/satellites-now-keep-track-backcountry-133300129.html

4. 💛 Half a million strangers flood bullied people with kindness
The Kindness Mob picks someone facing a wave of online hate and buries the trolls in compliments. Since 2023 it has helped about 400 people, like a woman mocked for a weight-loss video.
🔗 CBS News: https://www.cbsnews.com/news/kindness-mob-battles-online-bullying/

✨ Fun fact: River otters can live 8 to 15 years in the wild, and a female can have up to 6 pups in a litter.
