🦦 A river otter is back on California's San Mateo coast

An amateur naturalist's trail cameras on a 5,100-acre preserve caught a river otter 3 times, the first documented on that coast since the 1800s fur trade wiped them out.

Why it's cool: Cleaner creeks and protected land are letting otters return to places they vanished from.
