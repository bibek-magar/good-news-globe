🐠 'Sea muppets' look exactly like seahorses but aren't. Only 7 have ever been found, and none alive. Follow for 3 good-news wins a day

1. 🐠 Seahorse lookalikes turned out to be a whole new group
CT scans and DNA show 3 thumb-sized 'sea muppets' from southern Australia's deep reefs aren't seahorses at all. Evolution built the same body shape twice. A new species, the helmethead, was named.
🔗 ScienceDaily: https://www.sciencedaily.com/releases/2026/10/261029072119.htm

2. 🩺 Diet plus exercise put 54% of young adults' diabetes in remission
In a 96-person trial for type 2 diabetes under 45, a low-calorie diet with supervised workouts led 27 of 50 people to remission in 6 months, versus 2 of 46 on usual care.
🔗 ScienceDaily: https://www.sciencedaily.com/releases/2026/10/261029072129.htm

3. 🛰️ Spacewalkers added a 7th new solar wing to the space station
NASA's Anil Menon and Canada's Joshua Kutryk spent 7 hours 11 minutes outside installing a roll-out solar array. One more array will lift power from 160 kW to about 215 kW.
🔗 ScienceDaily: https://www.sciencedaily.com/releases/2026/10/261030002931.htm

4. 🗺️ An Indian scientist beat a shortcut limit that stood since 1996
Manoj Gupta at IIT Gandhinagar found a smarter way to estimate shortest routes across huge networks, keeping the 'at most double' accuracy promise even for nearby points, with no slowdown.
🔗 ScienceDaily: https://www.sciencedaily.com/releases/2026/10/261029072146.htm

✨ Fun fact: All 7 known sea muppets were caught by accident in nets or dredges 50 to 125 m down.
