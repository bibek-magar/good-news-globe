🐠 Seahorse lookalikes turned out to be a whole new group

CT scans and DNA show 3 thumb-sized 'sea muppets' from southern Australia's deep reefs aren't seahorses at all. Evolution built the same body shape twice. A new species, the helmethead, was named.

Why it's cool: An entire genus hid misfiled in museum drawers for decades.
