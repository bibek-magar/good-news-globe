🐒 The Bangka slow loris, a venomous primate missing from science since the 1930s, is alive in Indonesia + 3 more wins 👇

1. 🐒 A primate missing from science since the 1930s is alive
The Bangka slow loris was listed as 'possibly extinct'. But a rescue centre had saved 90+ of them from gardens, roads and power lines since 2015, and released 86 back to forest.
🔗 Phys.org: https://phys.org/news/2026-10-venomous-primate-thought-lost-science.html

2. ♻️ A cheap trick recycles Plexiglass back to new, up to 98%
ETH Zurich chemists broke acrylic glass back into its building blocks with one cheap chemical, no solvents, at about half the heat older methods need. Even dyed scraps came back pure.
🔗 Good News Network (@goodnewsnetwork): https://www.goodnewsnetwork.org/scientists-discover-way-to-recycle-acrylic-glass-into-brand-new-material/

3. 🪐 The solar system's biggest canyon is still quaking on Mars
A marsquake picked up by NASA's InSight lander came from a fault slipping 20-40 km beneath Valles Marineris. No impact or landslide could explain it.
🔗 Phys.org: https://phys.org/news/2026-10-giant-canyon-mars-tectonically-today.html

4. 🐶 A 10-year-old dog survived a month alone in Yellowstone
Luna, a Jack Russell, bolted from a cabin and spent over a month among bears and wolves. A volunteer caught her in a trap baited with her family's T-shirt and pillowcase.
🔗 Good News Network (@goodnewsnetwork): https://www.goodnewsnetwork.org/10-year-old-dog-found-month-lost-in-yellowstone/

✨ Fun fact: Valles Marineris is about 3,900 km long, up to 600 km wide and more than 9 km deep.
