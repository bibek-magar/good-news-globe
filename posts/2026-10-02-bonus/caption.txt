🦋 Australia's Bogong moths lost 99.5% of their numbers. Now swarms are filling the night sky again + 3 more wins 👇

1. 🦋 Moth swarms that once blocked out the stars are back
Drought, pesticides and habitat loss wiped out an estimated 99.5% of Australia's Bogong moths after 2017. This spring, scientists are seeing big migrating swarms again, likely helped by wetter weather.
🔗 The Straits Times (AFP) (@straits_times): https://www.straitstimes.com/asia/australianz/back-from-the-brink-australias-bogong-moths-are-again-swarming-the-skies

2. 🥚 A new stick insect lays eggs with tiny lids
University of Sydney scientists found 2 new giant stick insect species. One, from the endangered Mabi rainforest, lays eggs with a lid-like structure never recorded in any stick insect before.
🔗 ScienceDaily / Univ. of Sydney: https://www.sciencedaily.com/releases/2026/09/260930020315.htm

3. 🚀 Crew-13 makes the fastest US trip ever to the space station
SpaceX's Dragon reached the ISS just 7 hours 55 minutes after launch, a US record. The last crew took over 30 hours. Commander Jessica Watkins is the first Black woman to lead a crew to orbit.
🔗 DW (@dwnews): https://www.dw.com/en/new-crew-makes-the-quickest-us-flight-to-iss/a-79509556

4. 🏛️ A 2,800-year-old palace was built like a skyscraper
Computer models of the Grat Be'al Gibri palace in Ethiopia show its timber-laced stone walls could have carried about 16 storeys, double the 8 floors archaeologists had imagined.
🔗 HeritageDaily: https://www.heritagedaily.com/2026/10/ancient-ethiopian-palace-could-have-stood-16-storeys-high-study-finds/159472

✨ Fun fact: Bogong moth swarms once confused forecasters at the Sydney 2000 Olympics, who mistook them for rain on radar.
