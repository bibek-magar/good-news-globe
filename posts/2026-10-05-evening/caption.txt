1. 🦞 A 1-in-100-million white lobster joins a DNA study
Casper, a ghost-white lobster, moved from the Maine State Aquarium to the University of New England, where scientists are scanning 15,000+ genes to learn why some lobsters turn rare colours.

2. ⛪ Syria reopens a 1,500-year-old church after 14 years

3. 🌬️ Antarctica's wind turbines saved 122,000 gallons of diesel
Three turbines bolted to Ross Island's Crater Hill have powered NZ's Scott Base and the US McMurdo Station for 15 years in -30°C cold, avoiding about 1,200 tonnes of CO2.

4. 🐋 Oman's lonely humpbacks may be a whole new subspecies
Comparing DNA from 2,285 humpbacks worldwide, scientists found Arabian Sea whales have followed their own path for tens of thousands of years. Only about 82 are thought to live off Oman.
