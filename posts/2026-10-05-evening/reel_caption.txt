🦞 A 1-in-100-million white lobster joins a DNA study

Casper, a ghost-white lobster, moved from the Maine State Aquarium to the University of New England, where scientists are scanning 15,000+ genes to learn why some lobsters turn rare colours.

Why it's cool: Its DNA is collected without harm, and it fills the rarest gap in the colour spectrum.
