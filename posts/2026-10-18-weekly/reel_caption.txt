🐢 Hunted to under 1,000, giant tortoises now outnumber people

19th-century hunting left fewer than 1,000 tortoises on Aldabra. After decades of protection, a new count estimates about 183,000 grown tortoises, more than the Seychelles' whole human population.

Why it's cool: Proof that protecting a place for decades can bring a species all the way back.
