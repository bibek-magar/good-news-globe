💵 A barber found $128,514 on top of an ATM and returned it all

A bag stuffed with cash and 19 checks sat on a bank's ATM, apparently left by a repair technician. The man called police at once and handed it over. Police counted every dollar.

Why it's cool: A detective said nobody had ever turned in that much money before.
