1. 💵 A barber found $128,514 on top of an ATM and returned it all
A bag stuffed with cash and 19 checks sat on a bank's ATM, apparently left by a repair technician. The man called police at once and handed it over. Police counted every dollar.
🔗 Good News Network (@goodnewsnetwork): https://www.goodnewsnetwork.org/texas-man-finds-more-than-128000-on-top-of-a-banks-atm-and-returns-every-dollar/

2. ✋ A 46,000-year-old Neanderthal hand was found in a Spanish cave
6 bone fragments from Abauntz cave belong to a Neanderthal man's right hand, the first Neanderthal remains ever found in Navarre. His DNA survived well enough to study.
🔗 People (@people): https://people.com/46-000-year-old-bone-fragments-found-in-groundbreaking-discovery-12184511

3. 🥚 A rare egg from an extinct giant bird is going home to New Zealand
Found in 1966, the moa egg spent about 60 years in a US private collection. Colossal Biosciences bought it at auction to return it to Ngāi Tahu, the Māori people whose ancestors lived with moa.
🔗 Los Angeles Times: https://www.latimes.com/science/story/2026-10-20/biotech-firm-colossal-set-to-return-extinct-birds-egg-to-new-zealand

4. 🦍 A gorilla rescued from traffickers is now a mother in the wild
Lulingu, a critically endangered Grauer's gorilla, was saved from traffickers as a baby and raised at the GRACE sanctuary. Released near Mt. Tshiaberimu, she has given birth and is raising her baby.
🔗 AP via Yahoo News: https://www.yahoo.com/news/world/articles/reintroduced-grauers-gorilla-raising-baby-194810268.html

✨ Fun fact: The tallest moa could stand nearly 12 feet (3.6 m) tall with its neck stretched up.
