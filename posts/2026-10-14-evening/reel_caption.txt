🐆 A rare jaguar was photographed crossing a vital forest corridor

Panthera's camera traps caught a male jaguar in the corridor linking Guatemala and Honduras. Experts call photos like this a once-a-decade treat, proof big cats still use the route.

Why it's cool: Linked forests keep jaguar groups from becoming isolated and inbred.
