🐆 Camera traps caught a wild jaguar using the forest corridor between Guatemala and Honduras, a once-a-decade sight + 3 more wins 👇

1. 🐆 A rare jaguar was photographed crossing a vital forest corridor
Panthera's camera traps caught a male jaguar in the corridor linking Guatemala and Honduras. Experts call photos like this a once-a-decade treat, proof big cats still use the route.
🔗 PEOPLE: https://people.com/rare-photos-show-male-jaguar-in-guatemala-wildlife-corridor-12160157

2. 🏰 A 10th-century building turned up at Otto the Great's palace
Beside the royal palace at Helfta, diggers found a stone building with a cellar and a well-preserved staircase. Other finds include silver coins and an almost playable bone flute.
🔗 HeritageDaily: https://www.heritagedaily.com/2026/10/archaeologists-uncover-new-buildings-at-lost-royal-palace-of-otto-the-great/159554

3. 🧶 10 women, most over 70, knitted 567 dolls for children in need
Meeting monthly in Cape Town, the group knitted 567 IZZY Dolls with their own wool. 11 groups, including a cancer charity helping kids in chemo, will hand them out.
🔗 IOL: https://iol.co.za/lifestyle/2026-10-14-567-handmade-dolls-10-women-and-one-powerful-act-of-kindness/

4. 📡 The most distant radio flash ever traced was found with Webb
South Africa's MeerKAT caught a radio flash lasting milliseconds. A University of Sydney-led team used Webb to trace it to a tiny galaxy from when the universe was ~3 billion years old.
🔗 Mashable: https://mashable.com/science/james-webb-space-telescope-most-distant-fast-radio-burst-confirmed
