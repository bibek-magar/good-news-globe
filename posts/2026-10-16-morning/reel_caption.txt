🟢 Astronomers found the 1st green star ever seen

White dwarf WD J1330+6435 swallowed rocky debris from its old planets. Sodium in its dense atmosphere absorbs orange light, so to human eyes it would glow a ghostly green.

Why it's cool: No star, living or dead, had ever been credibly reported as green.
