🐊 Tiny cave crocs are filmed snatching bats for the 1st time

In Gabon's pitch-dark Abanda Caves, remote cameras caught dwarf crocodiles, the world's smallest, ambushing bats. Living in bat guano has tanned their skin orange, and they're genetically set apart from forest crocs.

Why it's cool: Gabon is working to protect the caves as a reserve, and this footage shows evolution in action.
