🐊 Tiny crocodiles living in a pitch-black cave in Gabon were filmed hunting bats for the first time + 3 more wins 👇

1. 🐊 Tiny cave crocs are filmed snatching bats for the 1st time
In Gabon's pitch-dark Abanda Caves, remote cameras caught dwarf crocodiles, the world's smallest, ambushing bats. Living in bat guano has tanned their skin orange, and they're genetically set apart from forest crocs.
🔗 Live Science: https://www.livescience.com/animals/alligators-crocodiles/watch-the-worlds-smallest-crocodiles-snatch-bats-in-their-hidden-underground-lair-a-behavior-never-seen-before

2. 🌱 Antarctica's only 2 flowering plants found 2 ways to cope
Chilean and Spanish scientists warmed Antarctic hairgrass and pearlwort under small open-top chambers for 7 years. One played it safe and saved water; the other boosted its photosynthesis and water flow.
🔗 Discover Wildlife: https://www.discoverwildlife.com/environment/flowering-plant-adaptation-antarctica

3. 🐻 A bear who rode on mom's back as a cub wins Fat Bear Week
Bear 89, 'Backpack', beat Bear 910 in a public online vote to win Fat Bear Week 2026. Badly hurt in one leg as a yearling in 2007, he recovered with his mom's help and grew into a gentle giant.
🔗 Popular Science: https://www.popsci.com/environment/backpack-fat-bear-week-winner-2026/

4. 🔭 Webb finds 'failed stars' just twice the mass of Jupiter
In the star nursery IC 348, NASA's Webb found brown dwarfs only twice Jupiter's mass, the lightest known and far smaller than theory predicts. One even has a disk where planets could form.
🔗 NASA Science: https://science.nasa.gov/missions/webb/nasas-webb-reveals-dynamic-panorama-of-star-formation/

✨ Fun fact: The lightest brown dwarfs Webb found weigh just 0.19% of the Sun's mass.
