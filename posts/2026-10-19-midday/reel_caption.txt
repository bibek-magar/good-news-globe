🏃‍♀️ At 93, a sprinter's muscle fibres match a woman in her 20s

Emma Mazzenga holds the over-90 world record for 200 m. Scientists found her fitness level matches someone in their 50s, and her fatigue-resistant muscle fibres are as big as a 20-something's.

Why it's cool: She restarted running at 53. Researchers hope she shows how much ageing is avoidable.
