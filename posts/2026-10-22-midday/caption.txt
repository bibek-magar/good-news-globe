💀 A skeleton found in a Roman coffin at Westminster Abbey in 1869 may finally have a name: a medieval abbot + 3 more wins 👇

1. 🏰 Westminster Abbey's mystery skeleton may be a medieval abbot
The skeleton in a Roman stone coffin dates to 987-1052 AD, centuries after the coffin was made. An expert now names 2 abbots who fit: Ælfwig, who died around 1020, or Wulfnoth.
🔗 People (@people): https://people.com/150-year-old-mystery-of-skeleton-found-in-roman-coffin-at-westminster-abbey-may-finally-be-solved-12186110

2. ☄️ A galaxy-mapping telescope found its 1st comet by accident
Euclid wasn't built to hunt comets, but a PhD student's software spotted one streaking through 5 sets of its images. The comet is about 1.3 billion km away and loops over the solar system.
🔗 BBC Sky at Night Magazine: https://www.skyatnightmagazine.com/news/comet-c-2026-s1-euclid

3. 🐅 A forest worker fought off a tiger with a stick to save his friend
On patrol in Corbett, Ramnath turned to see a tiger on top of his young colleague Pitambar. He hit it 3 times with his wooden stick; it let go, and Pitambar survived with bite wounds.
🔗 ThePrint (PTI) (@theprintindia): https://theprint.in/india/galat-jagah-pakad-liya-forest-guards-split-second-courage-saves-colleague-from-tiger/3078395

4. 🐋 After 17 months of care, an orphaned manatee swam free
Cullen was hit by a boat in May 2025 alongside his mother, who didn't survive. After rehab in Miami, the Keys and Jacksonville, he was released back into the sea at Tavernier.
🔗 WSVN 7News: https://wsvn.com/news/local/florida/rescued-manatee-returns-wild
