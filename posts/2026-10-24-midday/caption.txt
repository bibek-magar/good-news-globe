🪙 A Viking coin hoard in Norway grew from 19 coins to 5,500, with a king's coin not found since 1924 + 3 more wins you missed 👇

1. 🪙 A Viking hoard grew from 19 coins to a record 5,500
Metal detectorists found 19 silver coins at Mørstad in April. Digs have since raised it to 5,500, including a coin of King Olaf II, struck 1024–1028. Fewer than 20 are known.
🔗 Gizmodo: https://gizmodo.com/record-viking-hoard-yields-a-coin-not-found-in-over-100-years-2000829598

2. 🐍 A desert snake last recorded in 1872 was seen again
Forest officers on patrol near Bhuj photographed an Afro-Asian sand snake, the first confirmed record in Kutch since a naturalist described it there in 1872.
🔗 The Times of India: https://timesofindia.indiatimes.com/etimes/animals/a-snake-last-recorded-in-gujarats-kutch-in-1872-has-been-spotted-again-after-more-than-150-years-drawing-attention-to-the-regions-biodiversity-and-the-rare-reptiles-remarkable-rediscovery/articleshow/135160770.cms

3. 🧠 A drug-free PTSD treatment was cleared by the FDA
It maps each patient's brain waves, then targets magnetic pulses to match. The maker reports a 52% drop in symptoms and 68% remission in its trial; full results are still to come.
🔗 ScienceDaily: https://www.sciencedaily.com/releases/2026/10/261022081309.htm

4. 🐕 No trap could catch a stray dog, so a man walked her 90 miles
For a month, volunteers tried to rescue a wary dog living at a remote rest area. Then a hiker set off on foot with his own dog, and she followed them for 4 days, all the way to safety.
🔗 Cowboy State Daily: https://cowboystatedaily.com/2026/10/22/how-an-uncatchable-rest-area-dog-was-secretly-walked-90-miles-home-to-safety
