🪙 A Viking hoard grew from 19 coins to a record 5,500

Metal detectorists found 19 silver coins at Mørstad in April. Digs have since raised it to 5,500, including a coin of King Olaf II, struck 1024–1028. Fewer than 20 are known.

Why it's cool: It's the biggest such hoard found in Norway since 1866, and the coin may have been in the king's own hand.
