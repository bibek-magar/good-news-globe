🌕 NASA's GIMLI mission will hunt for a cave under the Moon

A lander and rover will use radar, seismic sensors, a gravimeter and cameras to see if the Marius Hills pit opens into a vast lava tube, and measure how big it is.

Why it's cool: A cave could shield future astronauts from radiation and wild temperature swings.
