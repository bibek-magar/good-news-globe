🌕 NASA picked a mission to find out if a deep pit on the Moon opens into a huge lava-tube cave + 3 more wins you missed 👇

1. 🌕 NASA's GIMLI mission will hunt for a cave under the Moon
A lander and rover will use radar, seismic sensors, a gravimeter and cameras to see if the Marius Hills pit opens into a vast lava tube, and measure how big it is.
🔗 ScienceDaily: https://www.sciencedaily.com/releases/2026/10/261003234013.htm

2. 🎺 Free music classes now reach 20,000+ kids in Miami
A local nonprofit teaches instruments for free in 131 schools across Miami-Dade. Some former students now lead the classes, like trumpet teacher Reinaldo Velez.
🔗 Good News Network (@goodnewsnetwork): https://www.goodnewsnetwork.org/nonprofit-brings-free-music-education-to-more-than-20000-miami-students-in-131-schools/

3. 🍎 A pomegranate compound may help stiff hearts relax
King's College London found urolithin A, made in the body after eating pomegranates, walnuts and some berries, improved the heart's ability to relax and cut scarring.
🔗 Good News Network (@goodnewsnetwork): https://www.goodnewsnetwork.org/pomegranates-walnuts-and-berries-could-unlock-heart-failure-treatment-with-urolithin-a/

4. ⚛️ China achieves the 1st fusion with clean hydrogen-boron fuel
Engineers at ENN Group briefly fused hydrogen and boron-11 in a spherical tokamak. The reaction makes helium and energy without the high-energy neutrons of standard fusion fuel.
🔗 Good News Network (@goodnewsnetwork): https://www.goodnewsnetwork.org/chinese-commercial-reactor-achieves-nuclear-fusion-with-clean-hydrogen-boron-fuel/

✨ Fun fact: Lava tubes are common on Earth; finding one on the Moon would let scientists use Earth caves as a guide.
