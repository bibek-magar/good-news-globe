1. 🪐 A Sahara meteorite is the oldest lava rock from Mars
Tiny zircon crystals date the meteorite Teghaza 001 to 4.1 billion years, the oldest Mars igneous rock found by a huge margin. It holds manganese oxide, a sign of wetter conditions.
🔗 Phys.org: https://phys.org/news/2026-10-rare-billion-year-martian-meteorite.html

2. 🔥 Charcoal shows Neanderthals tidied their campfires
At Abric Romaní, a rock shelter used from 110,000 to 40,000 years ago, mapped charcoal shows one main ash heap by the hearths and smaller piles, likely from cleaning them out.
🔗 Phys.org: https://phys.org/news/2026-10-ancient-charcoal-clues-neanderthal.html

3. 🚗 Students rebuilt a wrecked car and gave it to a veteran
Collision-repair students at Dunwoody College spent months fixing a crashed 2020 Hyundai Elantra, then handed it to Navy veteran Robert Hanson, who had been walking everywhere for food.
🔗 Good News Network: https://www.goodnewsnetwork.org/disabled-veteran-receives-donated-car-beautifully-refurbished-by-auto-collision-students/

4. 🌿 Camera traps found 607 species around Vietnam's My Son temples
30 camera traps in the forest around the ancient Cham temples caught Javan pangolins, civets, porcupines and monkeys. Surveys logged 607 animal species and 238 plant species.
🔗 Tien Phong via Vietnam.vn: https://www.vietnam.vn/en/bay-anh-he-lo-nhieu-loai-thu-quy-hiem-o-rung-my-son

✨ Fun fact: Of the 607 species logged at My Son, 189 are bottom-dwelling water animals.
