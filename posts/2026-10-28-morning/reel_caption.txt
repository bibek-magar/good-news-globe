🏺 A technician recreated the Lycurgus Cup's magic glass

The 4th-century Roman cup looks jade green in reflected light and ruby red with light behind it. After many experiments, Dr Lisa Hollands made glass that does the same, in quantity.

Why it's cool: Nobody knew exactly how the Romans did it. A new sculpture made from her glass goes on show in November.

🔗 University of Sheffield: https://sheffield.ac.uk/news/technician-cracks-1600-year-old-mystery-replicate-roman-masterpiece
