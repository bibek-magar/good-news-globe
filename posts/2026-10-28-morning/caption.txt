🏺 A Sheffield technician recreated the Lycurgus Cup's Roman glass, which glows green or ruby red, 1,600 years on + 3 more wins 👇

1. 🏺 A technician recreated the Lycurgus Cup's magic glass
The 4th-century Roman cup looks jade green in reflected light and ruby red with light behind it. After many experiments, Dr Lisa Hollands made glass that does the same, in quantity.
🔗 University of Sheffield: https://sheffield.ac.uk/news/technician-cracks-1600-year-old-mystery-replicate-roman-masterpiece

2. ☄️ Webb found ice from another star system like our own
Watching interstellar comet 3I/ATLAS 3 times, the James Webb telescope saw tiny grains of dusty water ice. They match icy worlds beyond Neptune.
🔗 Phys.org: https://phys.org/news/2026-10-webb-links-interstellar-comet-3iatlas.html

3. 🐒 Female bonobos turn out to be the main hunters
A 10-year study of 2 wild groups found females led 60% of hunts with known hunters, even mothers carrying babies. Older females caught forest antelopes 3 times as often as males.
🔗 Phys.org: https://phys.org/news/2026-10-bonobo-females-males-decade-reveals.html

4. 🚗 A stranger's wrong turn saved a big foster family
His GPS sent Casey down an unfamiliar road, where he saw a house on fire. He ran in, woke the dad and helped get 11 children and a grandchild out safely.
🔗 Good News Network: https://www.goodnewsnetwork.org/family-thats-fostered-500-children-saved-from-housefire-thanks-to-strangers-wrong-turn/

✨ Fun fact: The Lycurgus Cup is the only Roman glass cage cup known to change colour like this.
