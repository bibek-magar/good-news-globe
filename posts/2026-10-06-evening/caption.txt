🦗 New Zealand has released 11,000 wētāpunga, the world's heaviest insect, and their numbers are rising + 3 more wins you missed 👇

1. 🦗 The world's heaviest insect is officially bouncing back
A breeding programme has released 11,000 wētāpunga, and the species is now listed as increasing. Females can weigh as much as a mouse and grow up to 10 cm long.
🔗 The Guardian: https://www.theguardian.com/world/2026/oct/05/new-zealand-worlds-heaviest-insect-breeding-programme

2. 🚑 A solar-powered ambulance drove 800+ km across Kenya
Dutch students built Stella Juva, billed as the 1st solar ambulance. It carries vaccines, an ultrasound and X-ray gear, and is being trialled in Kenya with Amref Health Africa.
🔗 Geographical: https://geographical.co.uk/news/solar-ambulance-brings-healthcare-to-off-grid-communities

3. 🚲 A prosthetist turns old bike parts into free-to-copy legs
Ben Hogan's Project Bike Leg makes prosthetic legs almost entirely from used bike parts. He taught students in Ghana to build them and isn't patenting the design.
🔗 People: https://people.com/michigan-man-creates-prosthetic-legs-from-repurposed-bike-parts-12152651

4. 🐘 368 farmers gave up land so elephants could cross safely
Tanzania's first protected wildlife corridor links savanna and mountain rainforest. Cameras caught 529 elephants using it in 15 months, and 100,000 native trees have been planted.
🔗 Mongabay: https://news.mongabay.com/2026/09/tanzanias-first-official-wildlife-corridor-links-savanna-elephants-to-rainforest/
