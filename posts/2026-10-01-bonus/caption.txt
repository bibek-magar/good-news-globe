🏛️ Archaeologists in Greece are uncovering what's believed to be the school where Aristotle taught Alexander the Great + 3 more wins 👇

1. 🏛️ The likely school of Aristotle and Alexander emerges
At Mieza, archaeologists found lecture rooms with stone benches and a 1,700 sq ft complex with mosaic floors at the royal gymnasium where Aristotle is believed to have taught a young Alexander the Great.
🔗 Archaeology Magazine: https://archaeologymag.com/2026/09/macedonian-gymnasium-linked-to-alexander/

2. 🔴 Mars rocks were reshaped by water 3 separate times
NASA's Perseverance found that rocks at Jezero Crater's old lakeshore were altered by groundwater, lake water and later hot fluids, which left mineral veins up to 25 cm thick.
🔗 ScienceDaily: https://www.sciencedaily.com/releases/2026/09/260928100537.htm

3. 🐧 Scientists name the first new penguin in 100+ years
DNA revealed that gentoo penguins are really 4 separate species. The newly named southeastern gentoo, Pygoscelis kerguelensis, lives on the remote Kerguelen Islands.
🔗 The Washington Post: https://www.washingtonpost.com/science/2026/09/29/scientists-identify-new-species-penguin-first-more-than-century/

4. 🦈 Wild sharks heard a sound from 243 feet away
Filming blacktip sharks with a drone, researchers found they turned away from low-pitched sounds played up to 243 feet away. Across 209 trials with a high tone, they never reacted.
🔗 ScienceDaily: https://www.sciencedaily.com/releases/2026/09/260929053536.htm
