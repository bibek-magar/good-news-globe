🐯 In 2010 only about 3,200 wild tigers were left. Now there are roughly 5,700, a 70% rise, says WWF's new Living Planet Report + 3 more wins 👇

1. 🐯 Wild tigers have bounced back by 70% since 2010
In 2010 only around 3,200 wild tigers were left. WWF's 2026 Living Planet Report says there are now roughly 5,700, after years of sustained conservation work.
🔗 Euronews: https://www.euronews.com/2026/10/08/from-tigers-to-turtles-the-conservation-success-stories-that-prove-theres-still-hope-for-w

2. 🏺 A lost Hittite royal archive is giving up its secrets
Archaeologists recovered 131 clay tablets and fragments this season at Kayalıpınar, ancient Šamuḫa. They tell of an epidemic, a princess's funeral and generals begging the king for help.
🔗 Anatolian Archaeology: https://www.anatolianarchaeology.net/lost-hittite-royal-archive-reveals-3400-year-old-epidemic-princesss-death-and-military-crisis/

3. ☀️ Cuba now has 1.42 GW of solar power
Cuba has built 144 solar plants and expects to pass 1.5 GW by year's end. Renewables now make 21.4% of its electricity, and 646 solar pumps supply public water.
🔗 pv magazine: https://www.pv-magazine.com/2026/09/30/cubas-pv-capacity-tops-1-4-gw/

4. 🫐 A Nordic diet is linked to a longer life
Using health data from over 64,000 Swedish adults, Danish researchers estimate that a healthy Nordic diet of berries, rye, root veg and fish was tied to a 4% lower risk of death over 24 years.
🔗 ScienceAlert: https://www.sciencealert.com/ever-heard-of-the-nordic-diet-large-study-finds-it-could-help-you-live-longer
