🐯 Wild tigers have bounced back by 70% since 2010

In 2010 only around 3,200 wild tigers were left. WWF's 2026 Living Planet Report says there are now roughly 5,700, after years of sustained conservation work.

Why it's cool: It shows protection works, even for a species that looked doomed.
