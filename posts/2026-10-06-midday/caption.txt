🌊 Scientists are diving into Kyrgyzstan's Lake Issyk-Kul to study a Silk Road city that went underwater about 800 years ago + 3 more wins you missed 👇

1. 🌊 Divers explore a sunken Silk Road city in Kyrgyzstan
About 20 scientists and volunteer divers are studying a medieval trading city now under Lake Issyk-Kul. It is the first major exploration since the site was rediscovered in the 1800s.
🔗 Phys.org (AFP): https://phys.org/news/2026-10-sunken-silk-road-city-reveals.html

2. 🪐 Astronomers find a planet that may have formed from a dead star
A Jupiter-sized planet orbits the white dwarf HS 0209+0832 every 4.4 days. The star is rich in niobium, over 1,000 times the Sun's level, which hints the planet formed after the star died.
🔗 Phys.org: https://phys.org/news/2026-10-phoenix-planet-astronomers-world-reborn.html

3. 🧠 Medicine Nobel goes to the light switch for brain cells
Karl Deisseroth, Peter Hegemann and Georg Nagel won for optogenetics, which uses an algae protein to switch nerve cells on with light. It has already been tried in people to treat a form of blindness.
🔗 Phys.org: https://phys.org/news/2026-10-german-trio-medicine-nobel-optogenetics.html

4. 🦴 A new saber-toothed predator is named in Argentina
Fossils found on the pampas belong to Pampaluctor calibar, a marsupial relative that lived later than scientists thought its group survived. It was likely an ambush hunter.
🔗 Phys.org: https://phys.org/news/2026-10-saber-toothed-predator-argentina-lineage.html

✨ Fun fact: Issyk-Kul is one of the largest mountain lakes in the world.
