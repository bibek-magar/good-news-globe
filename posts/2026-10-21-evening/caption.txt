🦑 Wyoming fossils revealed the chunkiest 'squid' relative ever found, from 160 million years ago + 3 more wins you missed 👇

1. 🦑 Wyoming fossils revealed the 'fattest' squid relative ever
Most belemnite fossils in Wyoming are 1-2 cm wide. This new 160-million-year-old species is 6 cm across. Both known fossils are on show at the Wyoming Dinosaur Center.
🔗 ScienceDaily: https://www.sciencedaily.com/releases/2026/10/261019093434.htm

2. 🌌 Astronomers measured the Big Bang's helium more precisely than ever
In 130 hours of telescope time, a team studied 15 untouched galaxies to measure the helium made in the universe's first 5 minutes, to within 0.5%.
🔗 ScienceDaily: https://www.sciencedaily.com/releases/2026/10/261019093442.htm

3. 🦠 A soil microbe's weapon kills a dangerous gut bug and spares the good ones
Argyrin B, made by soil bacteria, cleared C. difficile in infected mice while barely touching helpful gut microbes. Taken by mouth, most of it stayed in the gut.
🔗 ScienceDaily: https://www.sciencedaily.com/releases/2026/10/261019093353.htm

4. 🐌 China's 1st known cave snail has no eyes and a see-through shell
Found 2,062 m up in a Yunnan cave, the tiny snail lives in total darkness and hides from light. It's named after a faceless god from an ancient Chinese text.
🔗 ScienceDaily: https://www.sciencedaily.com/releases/2026/10/261019093445.htm

✨ Fun fact: The new 'squid' fossil's name, Wyoteuthis, simply means 'a squid from Wyoming'.
