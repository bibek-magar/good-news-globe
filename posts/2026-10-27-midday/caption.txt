🕷️ Jumping spiders twitch their legs and eyes in a REM-like sleep stage, a new study filmed + 3 more wins you missed 👇

1. 🕷️ A jumping spider sleeps in 2 stages, one with twitches
Filming Portia fimbriata over many nights, a University of Bonn team saw a still, droopy phase, then a REM-like stage with leg curls and eye flickers while hanging upside down.
🔗 ABC News: https://www.abc.net.au/news/science/2026-10-27/jumping-spider-rem-sleep/107309370

2. 🌌 2 radio telescopes found a possible galaxy with no stars
China's FAST and the old Arecibo dish both detected AGC 322753: a rotating cloud with about 2 billion suns' worth of hydrogen, yet deep images show no matching glow.
🔗 Phys.org: https://phys.org/news/2026-10-fast-arecibo-dark-galaxy-vast.html

3. 🦎 A 99-million-year-old lizard tail proves an escape trick
An 18 mm piece of amber holds a lizard tail with a clean break and a ring of muscle, the same set-up modern lizards use to drop their tails and flee predators.
🔗 Phys.org: https://phys.org/news/2026-10-lizard-tail-amber-million-year.html

4. 🥐 She caught a tip-jar thief, then gave him a croissant
Café worker Luciana Gabriela Acevedo saw a man take her tips and made him give them back. Then she called him over and handed him a pastry instead of calling the police.
🔗 Fox News: https://www.foxnews.com/video/6407384515112

✨ Fun fact: Portia jumping spiders hunt other spiders, and an adult is only about 10 mm long.
