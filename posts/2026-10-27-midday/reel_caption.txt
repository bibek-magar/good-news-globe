🕷️ A jumping spider sleeps in 2 stages, one with twitches

Filming Portia fimbriata over many nights, a University of Bonn team saw a still, droopy phase, then a REM-like stage with leg curls and eye flickers while hanging upside down.

Why it's cool: An expert called it the most complete case for sleep in any spider, hinting sleep is very ancient.
