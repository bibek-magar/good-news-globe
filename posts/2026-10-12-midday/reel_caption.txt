🍲 Ukraine now feeds 3 million kids free hot school meals

Since September, pupils in grades 1-11 at municipal schools get a free hot lunch, about 3 million kids. In frontline Romny, one school cooks 350 meals a day and serves them in shelters during air raids.

Why it's cool: A hot meal brings kids to school and helps them learn, even in a war.
