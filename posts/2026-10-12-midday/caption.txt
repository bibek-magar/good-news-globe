🍲 Ukraine now gives 3 million kids free hot school meals, served even in air-raid shelters + 3 more wins 👇

1. 🍲 Ukraine now feeds 3 million kids free hot school meals
Since September, pupils in grades 1-11 at municipal schools get a free hot lunch, about 3 million kids. In frontline Romny, one school cooks 350 meals a day and serves them in shelters during air raids.
🔗 UNITED24 Media (@united24.media): https://united24media.com/life-in-ukraine/ukraine-feeds-3-million-schoolchildren-for-free-despite-russian-attacks-and-blackouts-23327

2. 🌱 Ethiopians planted 47 billion tree seedlings in 7 years
Tens of millions of volunteers joined the Green Legacy drive, which aims for 50 billion by 2030. A 2026 study of 44,500 seedlings planted in the south found about 80% had survived.
🔗 Grist: https://grist.org/solutions/fifty-billion-seedlings-can-ethiopia-replant-its-ravaged-forests/

3. 🦐 A giant sea 'woodlouse' in a drawer was a new species
A deep-sea isopod kept in a German university collection since the 1980s turned out to be unknown to science. Named Bathynomus tuebingoborikensis, it honours Tübingen and Puerto Rico's Taíno name.
🔗 PEOPLE: https://people.com/giant-isopod-kept-in-museum-for-decades-identified-as-new-species-12166377

4. 🐸 A frog lost since 1985 was found again in the Himalayas
Searching 14 sites for 424 hours, researchers found just two Chakrata torrent frogs, 65 km from where it was last seen, and read the species' DNA for the first time.
🔗 The Times of India: https://timesofindia.indiatimes.com/city/dehradun/critically-endangered-chakrata-torrent-frog-rediscovered-after-40-years-in-uttarkashi/articleshow/134876207.cms

✨ Fun fact: Giant isopods are cousins of garden woodlice, and some grow up to 50 cm long.
