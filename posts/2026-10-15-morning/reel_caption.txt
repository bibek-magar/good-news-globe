🐢 After 7 years of care, a rescued sea turtle goes home

Found on a Jeju beach in 2019, its shoulder and throat hurt by fishing gear, the loggerhead needed 7 surgeries at Aqua Planet Jeju. Now healthy, it returns to the sea near where it was found.

Why it's cool: Loggerheads are vulnerable, so every one returned to the ocean counts.
