🐢 A loggerhead sea turtle tangled in fishing nets in 2019 heads back to the sea after 7 surgeries + 3 more wins you missed 👇

1. 🐢 After 7 years of care, a rescued sea turtle goes home
Found on a Jeju beach in 2019, its shoulder and throat hurt by fishing gear, the loggerhead needed 7 surgeries at Aqua Planet Jeju. Now healthy, it returns to the sea near where it was found.
🔗 Korea JoongAng Daily: https://www.koreajoongangdaily.com/korea/loggerhead-sea-turtle-to-return-to-sea-after-seven-surgeries/12920271

2. 🏛️ A Roman temple to Mars was hiding just under a forest floor
At Kapersburg fort near Frankfurt, diggers found a village with workshops and a temple likely for Mars, plus pieces of a statue of Victoria. Untouched by farming, the forest kept the layers intact.
🔗 Good News Network: https://www.goodnewsnetwork.org/inches-below-the-forest-floor-archeologists-in-germany-unearthed-a-roman-temple-to-the-war-god/

3. 🦕 A 210-million-year-old dinosaur shows Zimbabwe's lost world
Musango, a 4.5 m, 220 kg plant-eater that walked on 2 legs, was found on Lake Kariba's shore. It and its relatives are unique to Zimbabwe, not copies of South Africa's dinosaurs.
🔗 ScienceDaily: https://www.sciencedaily.com/releases/2026/10/261012185013.htm

4. 🚲 A 17-year-old raised $30,000 to give kids bikes and lessons
Triathlete Jonathan Thai started Pedal Forward after a free bike workshop closed. He has handed out 400 donated bikes and 500 helmets, and his clinics now run in 3 US states.
🔗 Good News Network: https://www.goodnewsnetwork.org/teen-triathlete-raises-more-than-30000-to-donate-bikes-and-riding-lessons-to-low-income-families/

✨ Fun fact: Loggerheads are named for their big heads, which power jaws strong enough to crush shellfish.
