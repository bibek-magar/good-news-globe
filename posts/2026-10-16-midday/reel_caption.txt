🐶 A 10-year-old terrier survived 34 days lost in Yellowstone

Luna slipped out of a cabin and spent weeks dodging bears and wolves, even walking into lodges through automatic doors. Volunteers caught her in a trap baited with her family's clothes.

Why it's cool: Strangers, rangers and tourists all looked out for one little dog.
