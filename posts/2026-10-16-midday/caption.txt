🐶 A little dog survived 34 days lost in Yellowstone, among bears and wolves, and is finally home + 3 more wins you missed 👇

1. 🐶 A 10-year-old terrier survived 34 days lost in Yellowstone
Luna slipped out of a cabin and spent weeks dodging bears and wolves, even walking into lodges through automatic doors. Volunteers caught her in a trap baited with her family's clothes.
🔗 AP News: https://apnews.com/article/yellowstone-lost-dog-national-parks-24a0c2c74d7455ae5b07ba6de133ff52

2. 🏞️ South Africa created a new national park for its grasslands
Grasslands National Park protects 10,225 ha across 18 farms near Rhodes. Farmers keep their land but sign on to protect it, guarding rare birds and a key water source.
🔗 The Herald: https://theherald.co.za/news/2026-10-14-high-lying-corner-of-eastern-cape-home-to-sa-s-newest-national-park/

3. 📚 A former child worker now teaches school dropouts
Sanjali Soren nearly left school to work at brick kilns until a social worker helped her finish. Now she runs a centre in Asansol, teaching dropouts in their own Santhali language.
🔗 ETV Bharat: https://www.etvbharat.com/en/state/tribal-woman-opens-school-in-bengals-asansol-to-bring-dropouts-back-to-education-enn26101504414

4. 🛰️ Europe's new satellite sent its first stunning views of Earth
Just 2 weeks after launch, Sentinel-3C beamed back colour images of Italy and the Canadian Arctic. It will track sea ice, wildfires, drought and ocean heatwaves.
🔗 Good News Network (@goodnewsnetwork): https://www.goodnewsnetwork.org/esas-new-sentinel-3c-satellite-makes-its-debut-with-sumptuous-images-of-italy-and-the-arctic/
