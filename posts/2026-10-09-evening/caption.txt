1. 🌋 Kilauea has shot 54 lava fountains, and now we know why
Since December 2024, Hawaii's Kilauea has fired 54 separate lava fountains, more often than ever recorded. USGS scientists found trapped gas pushes the magma up and down like a piston.
🔗 Science News: https://www.sciencenews.org/article/kilauea-why-volcanoes-spew-fountains

2. 🪵 Neanderthals were skilled woodworkers, rare finds show
Near Barcelona, researchers documented 597 wooden pieces up to 62,000 years old, kept as moulds in mineral crust. 13 were shaped by Neanderthals, including spears and a club.
🔗 Phys.org: https://phys.org/news/2026-10-rare-wooden-tools-reveal-neanderthals.html

3. 💊 India plans to cut what patients pay for 110 cancer drugs
The government will cap trade margins at 30% on 110 costly cancer drugs. Officials say some prices could fall by up to 70%, after finding average markups of about 170%.
🔗 The Indian Express: https://indianexpress.com/article/health-wellness/government-cap-trade-margins-cancer-drugs-price-mrp-10912604/

4. ☀️ Solar has saved Europe €37.4 billion in gas imports
SolarPower Europe says sunshine cut gas demand enough to save €37.4 billion since gas prices spiked this year. In June, solar became the EU's largest power source, at 25%.
🔗 Euronews: https://www.euronews.com/2026/10/02/solar-saved-europe-3bn-in-fossil-fuel-imports-in-march-which-country-is-leading-the-way
