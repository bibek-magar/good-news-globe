🎶 Madagascar's singing lemurs open their mouths wider for high notes, just like opera sopranos + 3 more wins 👇

1. 🎶 Singing lemurs hit high notes the way sopranos do
Scientists filmed 19 wild indris in 83 videos and found they open their mouths wider as their pitch rises, the same 'formant tuning' trick opera sopranos use to sound louder without straining.
🔗 Smithsonian Magazine: https://www.smithsonianmag.com/smart-news/these-wild-lemurs-sing-just-like-opera-stars-widening-their-mouths-as-they-hit-the-high-notes-study-suggests-180989603/

2. 🔭 China's giant dish finds the lightest pair of dead stars yet
Using the FAST telescope, which has found about 900 pulsars, astronomers weighed a neutron-star pair at just 2.49 Suns combined, the lightest known. They whirl round each other every 2.36 hours.
🔗 Phys.org: https://phys.org/news/2026-09-astronomers-lowest-mass-neutron-star.html

3. 🏛️ Volunteers help unearth a rare Roman Venus statuette
Students, archaeologists and local history volunteers digging a Roman village near Budapest found a small lead statue of Venus, plus coins, brooches, a mirror fragment and an intact pair of iron tongs.
🔗 HeritageDaily: https://www.heritagedaily.com/2026/10/rare-venus-statuette-unearthed-at-roman-settlement-in-hungary/159475

4. 🧬 A drug built for one ALS patient shows early promise
Mayo Clinic and the nonprofit n-Lorem screened 320+ candidates to build a genetic medicine for a single patient with a rare form of ALS. A key marker of nerve damage responded quickly.
🔗 Medical Xpress: https://medicalxpress.com/news/2026-10-early-results-therapy-patient-rare.html

✨ Fun fact: The indri is the largest living lemur and the only lemur species known to sing.
