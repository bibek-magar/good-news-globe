🐶 Peggy Sue the bulldog weighed 70 lb and couldn't walk. Now she's 20 lb lighter and has a forever home + 3 more wins 👇

1. 🐶 A 70 lb bulldog who couldn't walk lost 20 lb and got adopted
Peggy Sue was so heavy the RSPCA had to carry her in a crate. A foster carer slowly built up her walks, and she dropped from 32.5 kg to 23.5 kg before a family in Taunton took her home.
🔗 Good News Network (@goodnewsnetwork): https://www.goodnewsnetwork.org/massive-70lb-dog-the-size-of-baby-hippo-sheds-lbs/

2. 🏺 3,000-year-old cups in Jordan may hold traces of Indian pepper
Chemists tested 7 burial cups from a tomb at Pella holding at least 82 people. 3 contained piperine, the compound in black pepper, a spice from India's Western Ghats, hinting at long-distance trade.
🔗 Arkeonews (@arkeo_news): https://arkeonews.net/3000-year-old-tomb-in-jordan-reveals-phoenician-ivory-rare-phrygian-belt-and-exotic-spices/

3. 🍓 Steam could replace toxic chemicals on strawberry farms
UC Santa Cruz researchers injected steam into strawberry beds to kill soil fungi, bacteria and weeds before planting. Farmers in the 4-farm trial reported bigger, healthier plants and more fruit.
🔗 Good News Network (@goodnewsnetwork): https://www.goodnewsnetwork.org/simple-soil-steaming-found-as-effective-replacement-for-chemicals-in-organic-strawberry-farming/

4. 💫 A star exploded in 1181, and it's still alive
Astronomers in Japan, China and the Arab world recorded a new light in 1181. Now Gemini North shows its remnant, Pa 30, in sharp detail, with the star that survived the blast sitting right at its centre.
🔗 Futurism (@futurism): https://futurism.com/space/weird-conditions-star-exploded-japanese-samurai
