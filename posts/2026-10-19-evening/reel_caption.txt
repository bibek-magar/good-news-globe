🐶 A 70 lb bulldog who couldn't walk lost 20 lb and got adopted

Peggy Sue was so heavy the RSPCA had to carry her in a crate. A foster carer slowly built up her walks, and she dropped from 32.5 kg to 23.5 kg before a family in Taunton took her home.

Why it's cool: Now she loves walks, car trips and splashing in streams.
