🐜 The asteroid that wiped out the dinosaurs may have helped ants take over the world, a study of 163 ant species finds + 3 more 👇

1. 🐜 The asteroid that ended the dinosaurs helped ants boom
Comparing the genomes of 163 ant species, scientists found bursts of 'jumping genes' about 66 million years ago, just before major ant groups split into thousands of species.
🔗 ScienceDaily: https://www.sciencedaily.com/releases/2026/10/261026225608.htm

2. 🌋 A lava planet keeps cool behind a 'sunscreen' of clouds
Watching TOI-561 b over 37 hours, the James Webb telescope found this molten world reflects about 75% of its starlight, likely from clouds of rock-like minerals in a thick atmosphere.
🔗 ScienceDaily: https://www.sciencedaily.com/releases/2026/10/261026225603.htm

3. 🌌 Scientists rebuilt the universe's first 2 billion years
Running on more than 15,000 graphics chips, the Lumina simulation models a cube of space about 1.5 billion light-years wide, over 100 times bigger than similar earlier projects.
🔗 ScienceDaily: https://www.sciencedaily.com/releases/2026/10/261026225600.htm

4. 🍖 A stranger secretly paid a $420 lunch for 27 people
