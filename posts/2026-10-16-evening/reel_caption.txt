🚢 A shipwreck up to 4,700 years old still holds its cargo

Divers off Türkiye's coast found a small Bronze Age boat with about 18 intact jars and 3 stone anchors. Dated to 4,400–4,700 years ago, it may be the oldest shipwreck ever found.

Why it's cool: It sailed around the time the Great Pyramid was built.
