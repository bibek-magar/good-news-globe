🚢 A shipwreck up to 4,700 years old was found off Türkiye with its cargo jars still intact + 3 more wins you missed 👇

1. 🚢 A shipwreck up to 4,700 years old still holds its cargo
Divers off Türkiye's coast found a small Bronze Age boat with about 18 intact jars and 3 stone anchors. Dated to 4,400–4,700 years ago, it may be the oldest shipwreck ever found.
🔗 Scientific American (@scientific_american): https://www.scientificamerican.com/article/scientists-discover-an-ancient-shipwreck-dating-to-when-the-great-pyramid-was-built/

2. 🪐 Hubble may have found a planet born from a dead star
A Jupiter-sized world may have formed from material cast off by a dying star, now a white dwarf. Clues in Hubble data from 1999 finally made sense after a new look.
🔗 EarthSky (@earthskyscience): https://earthsky.org/space/2nd-generation-planet-might-solve-an-old-hubble-mystery/

3. 🔍 A 6-year-old flipped a rock and found a rare ancient fossil
Leo Youngdahl hoped for a common fossil but found a conulariid, a jellyfish-like sea animal that lived 200–500 million years ago. Nebraska's only others were found over 55 years ago.
🔗 Nebraska Game and Parks: https://outdoornebraska.gov/about/press-events/news/6-year-old-finds-rare-fossil-at-schramm/

4. 🦌 A wildlife bridge cut animal crashes on a highway by 91%
Drivers once hit an animal about once a day on this stretch of I-25. In the 10 months since North America's largest wildlife overpass opened, they have hit just 2.
🔗 The Colorado Sun (@thecoloradosun): https://coloradosun.com/2026/10/14/i25-wildlife-overpass-collisions-drop/

✨ Fun fact: Amphorae, the jars found in the wreck, were the shipping containers of the ancient world for oil and wine.
