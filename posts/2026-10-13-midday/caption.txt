🐭 North America's tiniest mammal was thought extinct. Now 500+ Pacific pocket mice are back in the wild + 3 more wins 👇

1. 🐭 A mouse once thought extinct now has 3 new wild colonies
Pacific pocket mice, North America's smallest mammal, were thought extinct until 1993. San Diego Zoo has released 500+ since 2006, and a third new colony on a Marine Corps base is already breeding.
🔗 Popular Science: https://www.popsci.com/environment/pacific-pocket-mice-california/

2. 👁️ Common heart pills may help save sight from glaucoma
In a study of 420,000 people, glaucoma patients taking statins were 36% less likely to need incisional surgery within 5 years, and 55% less likely to need keyhole surgery, than those who didn't.
🔗 Good News Network: https://www.goodnewsnetwork.org/statin-drugs-may-protect-people-from-glaucoma/

3. 🏛️ A complete Roman window grille turned up, nails and all
At Augusta Raurica near Basel, diggers found an iron grille with five bars and two of its original nails still in place. Roman windows almost never survive whole, since wood rots and iron rusts.
🔗 La Brújula Verde: https://www.labrujulaverde.com/en/2026/10/archaeologists-unearth-a-complete-roman-window-grille-nails-and-all-at-augusta-raurica-the-oldest-colony-on-the-rhine/

4. 🙂 Hubble spotted a smiley face made of galaxies
In a new Hubble image, two orange galaxies form the eyes and a white galaxy forms the nose and smile. The round face comes from gravitational lensing, where a cluster's mass bends light into an arc.
🔗 FOX Weather: https://www.foxweather.com/earth-space/cluster-orange-white-galaxies-form-smiley-face-deep-universe-captured-hubble-space-telescope
