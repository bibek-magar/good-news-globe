🐭 A mouse once thought extinct now has 3 new wild colonies

Pacific pocket mice, North America's smallest mammal, were thought extinct until 1993. San Diego Zoo has released 500+ since 2006, and a third new colony on a Marine Corps base is already breeding.

Why it's cool: A species we nearly lost is spreading out again, one thumb-sized mouse at a time.
