🚀 4 volunteers have now spent 365 days sealed in NASA's 3D-printed Mars habitat + 3 more wins you missed 👇

1. 🚀 4 volunteers just passed a full year on a simulated Mars
Inside NASA's 1,700 sq ft 3D-printed habitat, the crew grew food, fixed staged breakdowns and took 'Marswalks', with messages to Earth delayed like real Mars calls.
🔗 India Today (@indiatoday): https://www.indiatoday.in/science/story/these-four-humans-have-spent-a-year-living-in-isolation-as-martians-on-earth-3021197-2026-10-20

2. 🪨 A rock that sank 90 km into Earth came back with a secret
Tiny crystals inside a Papua New Guinea rock show it plunged over 90 km down about 4 million years ago, hitting 800°C. It got hot surprisingly early on the way down.
🔗 ScienceDaily: https://www.sciencedaily.com/releases/2026/10/261020084816.htm

3. 🍊 A daily vitamin C pill was linked to better survival in a trial
In a trial of 109 people with blood conditions that can lead to leukemia, those on vitamin C had fewer adverse events and were more likely to be alive after about 3 years.
🔗 ScienceDaily: https://www.sciencedaily.com/releases/2026/10/261019093437.htm

4. 🐒 The world's 2nd-rarest ape grew to as many as 89
Rangers, villagers and Fauna & Flora counted 77 to 89 cao vit gibbons in about 14 families, up from 74. Babies were seen in 2024 and 2025.
🔗 Nature World News: https://www.natureworldnews.com/articles/73608/20261022/survey-finds-many-89-cao-vit-gibbons-hinting-worlds-second-rarest-ape-slowly-recovering.htm

✨ Fun fact: The world's 2nd-rarest ape was counted partly by song: each male gibbon has a unique call.
