🚀 4 volunteers just passed a full year on a simulated Mars

Inside NASA's 1,700 sq ft 3D-printed habitat, the crew grew food, fixed staged breakdowns and took 'Marswalks', with messages to Earth delayed like real Mars calls.

Why it's cool: Their data will help keep real Mars crews healthy. They step out on October 31.
