🥚 Adelaide Zoo's cassowaries laid bright green eggs, the zoo's first in 34 years + 3 more wins you missed 👇

1. 🥚 Adelaide Zoo's cassowaries laid their 1st eggs in 34 years
Martina, 10, and Jeffrey, 13, were paired for the 1st time and soon 2 bright green eggs appeared. Keepers don't know yet if they're fertile, but they're watching closely.
🔗 FOX via Yahoo News: https://www.yahoo.com/news/world/articles/adelaide-zoo-celebrates-1st-bright-013655949.html

2. 🧬 Blood cells from an 80-year-old were reset to under 20
Scientists turned an 80-year-old's blood cells into brain stem cells whose molecular age read under 20. The change happened slowly enough to track for over 100 days.
🔗 ScienceDaily: https://www.sciencedaily.com/releases/2026/10/261020085013.htm

3. 🚂 A 178-year-old solar storm mystery was solved by a typo
An 1871 report said a solar storm delayed an Exeter train by 16 minutes in 1841. But that rail line only opened in 1846. Old records show it really happened in 1848.
🔗 ScienceDaily: https://www.sciencedaily.com/releases/2026/10/261020085021.htm

4. ⛵ Strangers are helping an 86-year-old finish his 62-foot boat
Dale has welded a steel boat in his yard since 1987, dreaming of sailing to Alaska. 3 visitors from England started a fundraiser, and 1,500+ people gave over $41,000 in days.
🔗 Good News Network (@goodnewsnetwork): https://www.goodnewsnetwork.org/total-strangers-help-86-year-old-man-make-40-year-dream-reality/

✨ Fun fact: Dale's backyard boat already has 4 staterooms and is about 75% finished.
