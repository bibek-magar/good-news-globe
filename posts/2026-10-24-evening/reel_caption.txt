🐆 Camera traps caught one wild cat in 7 coat colours

Over 106 camera sites, scientists photographed Asiatic golden cats in golden, cinnamon, grey, black, brown, red and ocelot-spotted coats, all in one Himalayan district.

Why it's cool: It makes the Eastern Himalaya a hotspot for this cat's colours, and why it varies so much is still a mystery.
