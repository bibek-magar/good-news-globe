🐆 Camera traps in India caught one wild cat species in 7 different coat colours, from gold to black + 3 more wins you missed 👇

1. 🐆 Camera traps caught one wild cat in 7 coat colours
Over 106 camera sites, scientists photographed Asiatic golden cats in golden, cinnamon, grey, black, brown, red and ocelot-spotted coats, all in one Himalayan district.
🔗 NorthEast Now: https://nenow.in/environment/seven-shades-of-golden-cat-found-in-arunachal-study-reveals.html

2. 🐘 An 800,000-year-old elephant tooth revealed a lush ancient valley
A molar from a 10-tonne straight-tusked elephant, found beside stone hand axes, showed via CT scans, isotopes and pollen that early humans lived among reeds, willows and wetlands.
🔗 The Jerusalem Post: https://www.jpost.com/science/article-912524

3. 🌊 A TV reporter swam into a flood to save a trapped driver
While covering floods, ABC journalist Will Murray saw a man stuck in his sinking car. He swam out, a local named Daniel jumped in to help, and together they got the driver out.
🔗 Global News: https://globalnews.ca/news/12118698/australian-tv-reporter-rescues-man-sinking-car-video

4. 🐕 The Grand Canyon hired a dog to keep elk away from crowds
Blue, an 18-month-old Catahoula Leonberger, steers bold elk away from busy spots like the local school, without ever touching them. Rangers collared 9 elk to measure if it works.
🔗 KAIT: https://www.kait8.com/2026/10/23/grand-canyon-adds-new-dog-help-reduce-wildlife-interactions-with-visitors/

✨ Fun fact: The straight-tusked elephant was one of the largest land mammals of the Ice Age, weighing about 10 tonnes.
