🦧 53 orangutans were saved from Borneo's wildfires

Forest and peat fires are burning food supplies for critically endangered orangutans. Since August, the nonprofit YIARI has rescued 53, including mothers and infants in critical condition.

Why it's cool: Many have already been rewilded to safer forests, like Gunung Palung National Park, 5 hours away.
