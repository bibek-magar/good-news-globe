🦧 Since August, a Borneo rescue team has saved 53 orangutans from wildfires, and many are already back in the forest + 3 more wins you missed 👇

1. 🦧 53 orangutans were saved from Borneo's wildfires
Forest and peat fires are burning food supplies for critically endangered orangutans. Since August, the nonprofit YIARI has rescued 53, including mothers and infants in critical condition.
🔗 Good News Network: https://www.goodnewsnetwork.org/53-orangutans-rescued-from-fires-in-borneo/

2. 🍄 Scientists found 'D-rex', a giant new fungus in China
Dianjunus rex grows from the roots of cork oaks and can stand 50 cm tall and weigh 2.2 kg. That beats the old record of 20 to 30 cm, and it needed a whole new fungus family.
🔗 ScienceDaily: https://www.sciencedaily.com/releases/2026/10/261025073034.htm

3. ⚛️ A silicon quantum chip ran 5 qubits at once, accurately
Running many qubits together usually adds errors. RIKEN scientists found a hidden cause and a fix, keeping 5 silicon qubits at about 99.9% accuracy all at once.
🔗 ScienceDaily: https://www.sciencedaily.com/releases/2026/10/261024222024.htm

4. 🔥 Engineers built a cloak that hides objects from heat cameras
The 3D-printed aluminium and rubber shell guides heat around what is inside, so infrared cameras see nothing there, from any direction. It even hid shapes like human heads.
🔗 ScienceDaily: https://www.sciencedaily.com/releases/2026/10/261025073051.htm

✨ Fun fact: D-rex is named after the ancient Dian kingdom of Yunnan, and 'rex' for its king-sized build.
