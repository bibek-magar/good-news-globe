🐊 The asteroid wiped out the dinosaurs 66 million years ago, but crocodiles made it. 7,000+ fossils now suggest how. Follow for 3 good-news wins a day

1. 🐊 Why crocodiles outlived the dinosaurs
A UCL-led team studied 400+ species across 245 million years. Crocodiles always followed warm climates, which kept survivors in tropical refuges that stayed livable after the asteroid hit.
🔗 ScienceDaily: https://www.sciencedaily.com/releases/2026/10/261030002922.htm

2. 🐸 Four new frog species found in Peru
Biologists used body traits, mating calls and DNA to describe four new Oreobates frogs near Cordillera Azul National Park, taking the group from 27 to 31 known species.
🔗 Phys.org: https://phys.org/news/2026-10-frog-species-reveal-peru-biodiversity.html

3. 🔭 A glass chip made two telescopes work as one
A palm-sized chip from Heriot-Watt University combined starlight from two CHARA telescopes, a job that usually needs bulky mirrors and lenses. It even picked up fainter stars than the old system.
🔗 Phys.org: https://phys.org/news/2026-10-tiny-photonic-chip-links-telescopes.html

4. 🥔 Japan's potato island had its best harvest in a decade
A cooler summer with steady rain gave Hokkaido, which grows 80% of Japan's potatoes, denser, rounder spuds while heat hit crops across much of the Northern Hemisphere.
🔗 Good News Network: https://www.goodnewsnetwork.org/best-spud-harvest-in-a-decade-clears-the-gloom-of-crops-failing-from-scorching-summer/

✨ Fun fact: About 50 million years ago, crocodile relatives lived as far south as Antarctica.
