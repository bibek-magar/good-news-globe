🐊 Why crocodiles outlived the dinosaurs

A UCL-led team studied 400+ species across 245 million years. Crocodiles always followed warm climates, which kept survivors in tropical refuges that stayed livable after the asteroid hit.

Why it's cool: It helps solve a 66-million-year-old mystery, and shows today's crocodiles need room to move as the planet warms.
