🐘 An elephant orphanage that began with 3 calves freed 20

Zambia's Elephant Orphanage Project started in 2008 with 3 calves. It has now released 20 orphans into the wild, more than the 17 still in care, a first in its history.

Why it's cool: Chamilandu, orphaned by poachers, gave birth to her 2nd calf in the wild last year, with no human help.
