🐘 A Zambia elephant orphanage that began with 3 calves has now returned 20 orphans to the wild + 3 more wins you missed 👇

1. 🐘 An elephant orphanage that began with 3 calves freed 20
Zambia's Elephant Orphanage Project started in 2008 with 3 calves. It has now released 20 orphans into the wild, more than the 17 still in care, a first in its history.
🔗 Echo News: https://www.echo-news.co.uk/news/26655325.essex-conservationist-wins-bafta-honour-elephant-work/

2. 🌌 3 giant black holes were caught feeding in 1 ancient galaxy
In a galaxy seen just 1.2 billion years after the Big Bang, astronomers found 3 actively growing supermassive black holes. 2 of them sit only 620 light-years apart.
🔗 ScienceDaily: https://www.sciencedaily.com/releases/2026/10/261024222000.htm

3. 🤖 MIT built a gum-sized robot that swims on living muscle
Its thin gel body has 2 fins coated in muscle cells that contract when lit. By steering the light, the team guided it through a small underwater maze.
🔗 ScienceDaily: https://www.sciencedaily.com/releases/2026/10/261022081308.htm

4. 🕊️ A rescued NZ forest is drawing flocks of native pigeons
Since 2017, volunteers at Staveley Camp have pulled out hundreds of invasive trees. Native plants are regrowing, and kererū counts rose from 2 or 3 birds for 6 years to 7 in 2025.
🔗 The Times of India: https://timesofindia.indiatimes.com/science/wildlife/new-zealand-began-removing-invasive-weeds-from-staveley-camp-forest-in-2017-years-of-monitoring-later-found-native-regeneration-and-growing-birdlife/articleshow/135091323.cms

✨ Fun fact: Elephants walk so quietly that, as the orphanage's co-founder says, they can appear right beside you unnoticed.
