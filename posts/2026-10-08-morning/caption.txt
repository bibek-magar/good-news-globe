🐢 Jonathan the giant tortoise is at least 194, and scientists just found clues to his long life in his DNA + 3 more wins you missed 👇

1. 🐢 A 194-year-old tortoise's DNA hints at his long-life secret
Scientists read the genome of Jonathan, the oldest known land animal. In 272 of his genes, the chemical on/off switches stayed as orderly as a young tortoise's. He also has unique variants in 287 genes.
🔗 Scientific American: https://www.scientificamerican.com/article/scientists-unlock-the-longevity-secrets-of-194-year-old-jonathan-the-giant-tortoise/

2. 🛁 A 30-minute hot bath lowered blood pressure more than cycling
In a small study of 25 adults aged 45 to 70, a 30-minute soak at 40°C widened blood vessels and lowered blood pressure more than 30 minutes of moderate cycling.
🔗 ScienceAlert: https://www.sciencealert.com/a-hot-bath-may-lower-your-blood-pressure-more-than-a-30-minute-workout-can-study-reveals

3. ☀️ Pakistan brought in about 41 GW of solar panels in 3 years
Pakistan imported about 7.6 GW of solar panels in 2023, 16.4 GW in 2024 and 16.9 GW in 2025. Most were bought by households, farms, shops and factories, not the government.
🔗 The Friday Times: https://www.thefridaytimes.com/07-Oct-2026/pakistan-s-solar-boom-moving-faster-power-grid

4. 💍 Divers found a ring engraved with the Lighthouse of Alexandria
Underwater archaeologists found a bronze signet ring showing the lighthouse at the sunken palace of Antirhodos. It is the first known engraving of the Wonder on a signet ring.
🔗 The National: https://www.thenationalnews.com/news/mena/2026/10/07/new-image-of-an-ancient-wonder-divers-find-ring-showing-lighthouse-of-alexandria-at-sunken-palace/

✨ Fun fact: The Lighthouse of Alexandria is thought to have stood more than 100 metres tall.
