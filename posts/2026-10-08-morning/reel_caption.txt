🐢 A 194-year-old tortoise's DNA hints at his long-life secret

Scientists read the genome of Jonathan, the oldest known land animal. In 272 of his genes, the chemical on/off switches stayed as orderly as a young tortoise's. He also has unique variants in 287 genes.

Why it's cool: Learning how Jonathan stays healthy could inform research on human ageing.
