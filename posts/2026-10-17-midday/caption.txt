1. 🦩 A 60-year-old flamingo named Queen just hatched 2 chicks
Queen, a Chilean flamingo at the Louisville Zoo, is believed to be the oldest female to hatch chicks at an accredited US zoo. The previous record holder was 50.
🔗 WCVB: https://www.wcvb.com/article/60-year-old-flamingo-hatches-two-chicks-kentucky-zoo/74138760

2. 🔭 4 giant telescopes to catch cosmic blasts are now built
On a La Palma mountaintop, 4 telescopes with 23 m mirrors are complete. Each weighs about 100 tonnes yet can swing across the sky in 20 seconds to chase sudden gamma-ray flashes.
🔗 The Brighter Side of News: https://www.thebrighterside.news/post/new-large-sized-telescope-array-in-spain-ushers-in-a-new-era-in-exploring-the-extreme-universe/

3. 🦴 A 13,800-year-old camp may hold the Americas' oldest home
Near Fairbanks, diggers found thousands of artifacts, what may be the oldest dwelling and needles in the Western Hemisphere, and bones that could be the Americas' earliest pet dogs.
🔗 Live Science (@live_science): https://www.livescience.com/archaeology/americas/archaeologists-discover-the-oldest-dwelling-in-the-western-hemisphere-at-a-13-800-year-old-site-in-alaska

4. 🎓 She cleaned a campus for 26 years. Now her daughter is Dr.
Ranu Devi started as a sanitation worker at BIT Mesra on ₹400 a week and kept her 3 kids in school. Her daughter Aarti has now earned an engineering PhD at the very same institute.
