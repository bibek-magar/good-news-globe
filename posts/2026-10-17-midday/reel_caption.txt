🦩 A 60-year-old flamingo named Queen just hatched 2 chicks

Queen, a Chilean flamingo at the Louisville Zoo, is believed to be the oldest female to hatch chicks at an accredited US zoo. The previous record holder was 50.

Why it's cool: Flamingos can skip nesting for years, so a healthy pair of chicks at 60 is rare.
