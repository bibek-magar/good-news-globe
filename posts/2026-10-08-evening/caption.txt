⭐ A disease killed about 6 billion sunflower sea stars. Lab-raised ones just hunted urchins in Monterey Bay + 3 more wins 👇

1. ⭐ Lab-raised sunflower sea stars hunted urchins in the wild
In a test release, a dozen-plus lab-raised stars quickly ate sea urchins in Monterey Bay. A disease killed about 6 billion of them, and since then urchins have chewed through kelp forests.
🔗 SFGATE: https://www.sfgate.com/centralcoast/article/sunflower-star-release-monterey-22463590.php

2. 🌌 Telescopes caught gamma rays from a blazar 8 billion light-years away
The LST-1 and MAGIC telescopes picked up high-energy gamma rays from blazar OP 313, a black hole firing a jet straight at Earth. We see it as it was 5 billion years after the Big Bang.
🔗 Space.com: https://www.space.com/astronomy/black-holes/astronomers-detect-most-distant-supermassive-black-hole-powered-blazar-ever-a-cosmic-beacon-from-the-dawn-of-time

3. 🏃‍♀️ A 39-year-old mum ran 1,600 m barefoot in a saree
Kavita, a widowed daily-wage worker with 2 kids, trains each morning for a Home Guard job. In a trial run she finished 1,600 metres in 6 minutes 45 seconds, barefoot and in a saree.
🔗 ETV Bharat: https://www.etvbharat.com/en/offbeat/jharkhand-ramgarh-kavita-barefoot-saree-run-for-home-guard-recruitment-enn26100701055

4. 🎒 A teacher treks 5 km through leopard forest to teach 2 girls
For 13 years Jeevan Mithari has walked a steep forest trail with gaurs, wild dogs and leopards to a hamlet school. Today it has 2 students: sisters aged 8 and 5. The older one wants to be a doctor.
🔗 Times of India: https://timesofindia.indiatimes.com/city/pune/kolhapur-teacher-treks-5km-through-dense-forest-one-way-to-keep-two-girls-in-school/articleshow/133775671.cms
