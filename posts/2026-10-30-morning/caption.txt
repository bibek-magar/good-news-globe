1. 🐘 A giant elephant's tooth reveals a lost Ice Age oasis
Found next to stone handaxes at Gesher Benot Ya'aqov, a straight-tusked elephant's molar showed rivers, wetlands and woods that fed huge animals and early humans.
🔗 ScienceDaily: https://www.sciencedaily.com/releases/2026/10/261028232709.htm

2. 🧊 Ice in Tibet turned out to be over 128,000 years old
Cores drilled 23 years apart, in 1992 and 2015, matched closely. New dating puts the Guliya ice beyond 128,000 years, over 100,000 years older than the region's known record.
🔗 ScienceDaily: https://www.sciencedaily.com/releases/2026/10/261028232713.htm

3. 🍒 Pure cranberry juice slowed tooth plaque in a small trial
Children and adults who swished unsweetened 100% cranberry juice saw slower plaque regrowth and less acid, which harms enamel. Sugary juice drinks and water didn't do the same.
🔗 ScienceDaily: https://www.sciencedaily.com/releases/2026/10/261028232701.htm

4. ⚡ 30 years after it was predicted, a carbon 'metal' was built
Chemists at USTC built ultrathin ribbons of only 5- and 7-sided carbon rings on silver. Lifted with a microscope tip, one version carried current like a true metal.
🔗 Phys.org: https://phys.org/news/2026-10-ultrathin-carbon-ribbons-electricity-metals.html

✨ Fun fact: The Guliya ice cores were drilled 23 years apart, yet their climate signals matched.
