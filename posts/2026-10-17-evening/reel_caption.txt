🌊 Ranger divers found stone tools the sea swallowed 9,000 years ago

Aboriginal rangers trained as divers helped find 20+ stone tools at 2 sunken sites, 11–15 m deep. Some stone came from about 23 km away, carried across land that is now seabed.

Why it's cool: Rangers found a sunken spring their songlines still sing about.
