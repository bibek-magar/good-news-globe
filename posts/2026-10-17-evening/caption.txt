🌊 Divers off Australia found stone tools on land the sea swallowed at least 9,000 years ago + 3 more wins you missed 👇

1. 🌊 Ranger divers found stone tools the sea swallowed 9,000 years ago
Aboriginal rangers trained as divers helped find 20+ stone tools at 2 sunken sites, 11–15 m deep. Some stone came from about 23 km away, carried across land that is now seabed.
🔗 ABC News (@abcnews_au): https://www.abc.net.au/news/2026-10-14/newly-discovered-underwater-sites-off-burrup-peninsula-in-wa/107255826

2. 🐚 A 13-year-old's seashell-inspired fix for acidic oceans won $25,000
Raji Doshi paired algae with aragonite, the mineral in seashells, to soak up CO2 and buffer seawater. Her lab tests cut dissolved CO2, winning the 2026 3M Young Scientist Challenge.
🔗 The American Bazaar: https://americanbazaaronline.com/2026/10/15/indian-american-teen-doshi-named-americas-top-young-scientist-490055/

3. 🚶 Teens who walk more show better mental health, big study finds
Researchers tracked 5,515 young people with Fitbits for a year. Those under 5,000 steps a day had the most symptoms; those at 12,000+ had the fewest and thicker tissue in key brain areas.
🔗 Positive News (@positivenewsuk): https://www.positive.news/society/good-news-stories-from-week-42-of-2026/

4. 🍽️ London's oldest restaurant was given away to save it for good
Owner J-P Mayhew donated Rules, open for 228 years and loved by Charles Dickens, to the National Trust. The restaurant keeps trading, but its building is now protected for future generations.
🔗 Positive News (@positivenewsuk): https://www.positive.news/society/good-news-stories-from-week-42-of-2026/
