🐴 Night divers in Cork filmed a wild long-snouted seahorse, believed to be a 1st for Irish waters + 3 more wins 👇

1. 🐴 Night divers filmed a rare seahorse in Ireland's seagrass
A long-snouted seahorse just 4-5 cm long was spotted in Cork seagrass. Ireland has only 8 earlier seahorse records, the first in 1837 and most over 100 years old.
🔗 The Irish Times: https://www.irishtimes.com/environment/2026/10/22/night-divers-in-cork-make-rare-sighting-of-long-snouted-seahorse/

2. 🎲 Native Americans were rolling dice 12,800 years ago
Two-sided bone dice from Ice Age sites predate the oldest Old World dice by 6,000+ years. A researcher identified 600+ dice at 57 sites across 12 US states.
🔗 Good News Network (@goodnewsnetwork): https://www.goodnewsnetwork.org/native-americans-were-making-dice-and-gaming-thousands-of-years-before-anyone-else/

3. 🥥 Coconut waste fuel ran a jet engine as well as kerosene
Osaka chemists turned discarded coconut into jet fuel at low heat. Blended up to 50% with kerosene, it matched kerosene's efficiency and cut unburned hydrocarbons by 5-40%.
🔗 Good News Network (@goodnewsnetwork): https://www.goodnewsnetwork.org/coconut-oil-could-make-great-sustainable-jet-fuel-pioneering-experiments-demonstrate/

4. 🐦 Rats and pigs are gone, and a Samoan island is blooming
After 12 expeditions and 130+ days removing invasive rats and pigs, seedlings now carpet the forest. A month later, rare shy ground doves were nesting with eggs.
🔗 Good News Network (@goodnewsnetwork): https://www.goodnewsnetwork.org/samoan-island-witnesses-explosion-of-native-wildlife-following-removal-of-invasive-rats-and-pigs/

✨ Fun fact: The ancient dice were 2-sided, like a coin: one marked face counted as the scoring side.
