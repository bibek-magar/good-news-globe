🐴 Night divers filmed a rare seahorse in Ireland's seagrass

A long-snouted seahorse just 4-5 cm long was spotted in Cork seagrass. Ireland has only 8 earlier seahorse records, the first in 1837 and most over 100 years old.

Why it's cool: It's believed to be the 1st time a wild seahorse has been caught on video in Irish waters.
