🐕 1,200 years ago, Peru already had hairless dogs: a dog mummy from a Wari Empire site still has its bald skin and ears + 3 more wins 👇

1. 🐕 A 1,200-year-old dog mummy still has its hairless skin and ears
At a Wari Empire site north of Lima, scientists studied bones from at least 19 dogs. One naturally mummified skull, painted red with cinnabar, kept its hairless skin and ears.
🔗 ScienceDaily: https://www.sciencedaily.com/releases/2026/10/261016070644.htm

2. 🪱 Deep-sea 'spaghetti worms' with suckers get a new genus
Worms collected 467 to 674 m down off Christmas Island and the Cocos (Keeling) Islands have sucker-like pads no other spaghetti worm has. DNA showed they form a whole new genus.
🔗 ScienceAlert (@sciencealert): https://www.sciencealert.com/new-genus-of-nightmare-spaghetti-worms-found-deep-under-the-indian-ocean

3. 🔥 A retired couple's broken furnace was replaced for free
Diane and Brian Johnsen's old system couldn't be fixed, and Diane was working 3 jobs to cover the mortgage. Lennox's Feel the Love program and Aviator Heating & Cooling installed a new one at no cost.
🔗 Good News Network (@goodnewsnetwork): https://www.goodnewsnetwork.org/retired-couple-surprised-with-new-hvac-system-worth-16k/

4. 🧶 A village knitted fake traffic cops, and drivers hit the brakes
After crashes near a primary school, Fillongley's Knit and Natter group made life-size knitted officers and speed cameras. The mayor says you can see brake lights come on as cars pass.
🔗 Good News Network (@goodnewsnetwork): https://www.goodnewsnetwork.org/village-installs-knitted-traffic-cops-to-slow-speeding-drivers/
