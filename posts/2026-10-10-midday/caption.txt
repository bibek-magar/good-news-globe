🌱 Women in Nigeria's Niger Delta replanted 3 million mangroves on oil-damaged land, and fish are back + 3 more wins 👇

1. 🌱 Women replant 3 million mangroves on oil-hit Niger Delta land
Since 2018, teams led by Martha Agbani have replanted 3 million mangroves and restored 190 hectares of swamp in oil-scarred Ogoniland. Fish and crabs are returning.
🔗 The Guardian: https://www.theguardian.com/global-development/2026/oct/02/one-green-shoot-at-a-time-the-women-restoring-mangrove-forests-in-the-oil-ravaged-niger-delta

2. 🌕 NASA picks a mission to look for a cave under the Moon
GIMLI will land by the Marius Hills pit and use radar, seismic sensors and gravity readings to check for an old lava tube that could shelter future astronauts.
🔗 Universe Today: https://www.universetoday.com/articles/nasa-selects-gimli-to-search-the-marius-lunar-pit-for-a-potential-lunar-base-site

3. 🧬 Scientists find a hidden cause of inherited blindness
Mild faults in the IDUA gene, known for a severe childhood illness, explained sight loss in 14 otherwise healthy adults with retinitis pigmentosa.
🔗 University of Manchester: https://www.manchester.ac.uk/about/news/gene-link-to-inherited-blindness-discovered/

4. 🐶 A New Yorker carries shelter dogs around the city in a backpack
Bryan Reisberg has given more than 55 adoptable dogs a backpack tour of New York and shared each ride online, so shelter pups get seen by possible owners.
🔗 ABC7 New York: https://abc7ny.com/post/bryan-reisbergs-nyc-dog-backpack-rides-go-viral-help-shelter-pups-find-homes/19914214/
