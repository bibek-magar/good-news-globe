🌱 Women replant 3 million mangroves on oil-hit Niger Delta land

Since 2018, teams led by Martha Agbani have replanted 3 million mangroves and restored 190 hectares of swamp in oil-scarred Ogoniland. Fish and crabs are returning.

Why it's cool: Local women are healing their own land and getting their food and income back.
