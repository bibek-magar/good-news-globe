In Kenya, a solar-powered shipping container now delivers up to 400 dialysis sessions a month 🏥☀️ + 3 more wins you probably missed 👇

1. A shipping container becomes a rural dialysis clinic
Renal Roads turned a 40-foot solar-powered container into a mobile clinic with 4 dialysis machines. It delivers up to 400 sessions a month and is a finalist for the 2026 Africa Prize.
Source: TechTrends Kenya (https://techtrendske.co.ke/2026/09/28/kenyan-healthcare-innovators-africa-prize-final/)

2. Enceladus' icy spray sorts its own ocean chemistry
Studying 961 ice-grain readings from NASA's Cassini, scientists found that slow freezing separates salts into different grains before they shoot into space.
Source: ScienceDaily (https://www.sciencedaily.com/releases/2026/09/260929053528.htm)

3. 13,000-year-old cut marks reveal Chile's early hunters
Stone tools and cut marks on bones of gomphotheres, extinct tusked giants, show people were butchering them 12,000 to 13,000 years ago.
Source: Archaeology Magazine (https://archaeology.org/news/2026/09/29/butchered-bones-in-chile-dated-to-13000-years-ago/)

4. War-zone giraffes kept a surprisingly healthy gene pool
DNA from 30 Nubian giraffes shows high genetic diversity and low inbreeding, even after decades of conflict shrank the herd to about 235 animals.
Source: Mongabay (https://news.mongabay.com/short-article/2026/09/in-war-torn-south-sudan-giraffes-retain-a-surprisingly-healthy-gene-pool/)

✨ Fun fact: Enceladus' geysers spray so much ice that they feed one of Saturn's rings, the E ring.
