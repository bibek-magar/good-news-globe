🐦 A 'lost' bird nobody had photographed in 13 years was just filmed for the first time in Côte d'Ivoire + 3 more wins 👇

1. 🐦 A bird lost for 13 years is filmed for the first time
Crossing a small rainforest bridge, two bird surveyors spotted a yellow, orange and black Ghana cuckooshrike, unseen since 2013, and took the first known video of it on a 6-month West Africa trip.
🔗 PetaPixel (@petapixel): https://petapixel.com/2026/10/01/first-ever-video-of-a-ghana-cuckooshrike-after-13-years-as-a-lost-bird-species/

2. 🏺 4 mummies untouched for 1,000+ years rethink a conquest
In southern Peru, archaeologists found four Nasca mummies buried beside a Wari temple, not in the cemetery, with local food, pottery and alpaca offerings, hinting the 2 cultures worked together.
🔗 Gizmodo: https://gizmodo.com/archaeologists-found-4-nazca-mummies-in-the-wrong-place-it-could-turn-the-story-of-an-ancient-empire-upside-down-2000820434

3. 🍓 A fabric born from army uniform research triples strawberries
NC State's 'Plant Armor' cover keeps bugs off plants but lets sun, rain and air through. Covered strawberries gave up to 3.56 times more fruit in tunnel field tests, and it kept out every aphid in lab tests.
🔗 EurekAlert! / NC State: https://www.eurekalert.org/news-releases/1145923

4. ❤️ Stem-cell heart patches reach their 1st patient after approval
Surgeons in Japan placed sheets of heart muscle cells made from iPS stem cells on the heart of a woman in her 50s, the world's first surgery using the newly approved, commercially made sheets.
🔗 The Japan Times (@thejapantimes): https://www.japantimes.co.jp/news/2026/09/30/japan/science-health/heart-stem-cells-surgery/
