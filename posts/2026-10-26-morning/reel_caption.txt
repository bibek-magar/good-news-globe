🐾 A rare lynx was caught on camera in New Mexico

A PhD student checking photos from 120 trail-camera sites in Carson National Forest found a Canada lynx, the first documented in New Mexico in over 25 years. Experts confirmed it.

Why it's cool: Lynx are threatened in the US, and the find could shape how their snowy spruce-fir forests are managed.
