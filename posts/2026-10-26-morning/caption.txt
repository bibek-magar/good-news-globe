🐾 A Canada lynx was caught on a trail camera in New Mexico, the first documented there in 25+ years + 3 more wins you missed 👇

1. 🐾 A rare lynx was caught on camera in New Mexico
A PhD student checking photos from 120 trail-camera sites in Carson National Forest found a Canada lynx, the first documented in New Mexico in over 25 years. Experts confirmed it.
🔗 KFOX14: https://kfoxtv.com/news/local/nmsu-researcher-finds-rare-wild-cat-on-camera-first-documented-in-nm-in-over-25-years

2. 🐍 Tiny snakes escape danger by rolling like wheels
Dwarf reedsnakes in Borneo cartwheel away from threats at up to 3.69 m per second, about 4 times their top slithering speed, while keeping their heads steady and facing forward.
🔗 ScienceDaily: https://www.sciencedaily.com/releases/2026/10/261023023718.htm

3. 🌙 Astronomers may have found the 1st moon outside our Solar System
Using ESO's Very Large Telescope, a team spotted a wobble revealing a Jupiter-mass object circling a brown dwarf, which itself orbits a star in the CD-35 2722 system.
🔗 ScienceDaily: https://www.sciencedaily.com/releases/2026/10/261023023736.htm

4. 🚢 The world's oldest known shipwreck was found off Türkiye
Divers near Kekova Island found the cargo of a 10 m Bronze Age ship that sank 4,400 to 4,700 years ago: 30+ clay jars, some still holding residue, and 3 stone anchors.
🔗 The Times of India: https://timesofindia.indiatimes.com/world/rest-of-world/worlds-oldest-shipwreck-discovered-off-turkey-with-nearly-4700-year-old-pottery-revealing-clues-to-ancient-mediterranean-trade/articleshow/134995634.cms

✨ Fun fact: Some jars from the 4,700-year-old shipwreck still hold a thick, glossy residue from their ancient cargo.
