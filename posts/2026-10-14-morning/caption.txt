🦖 A dinosaur skull sat in a museum drawer for ~100 years. It's a new species, and Europe's 1st megaraptor + 3 more wins 👇

1. 🦖 A skull in a museum drawer since 1924 is a new dinosaur
Invictibellatrix amberina, a 3-4 m hunter with claws up to 20 cm, lived 120 million years ago. It is the first megaraptor, a T. rex relative, ever found in Europe.
🔗 Phys.org: https://phys.org/news/2026-10-isle-wight-fossil-europe-clawed.html

2. ☀️ 436 Korean villages will share income from their own solar
Residents co-own local solar projects and split the revenue, which pays for higher incomes, welfare and community projects. One region alone now has 125 villages signed up.
🔗 Seoul Economic Daily: https://en.sedaily.com/society/2026/10/13/jeonnam-gwangju-picks-125-solar-income-villages-most-in

3. 🐊 A 170-million-year-old croc relative had needle-thin teeth
Fossils from Morocco's Atlas Mountains revealed Ctenosuchus tenuidens, a long-snouted reptile whose unusually thin teeth suggest it hunted shrimp and small fish.
🔗 BBC News: https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/c6vg0px1dkmmo

4. 🍄 Meet 'D-rex', a giant new fungus that stands 50 cm tall
Found on oak roots in Yunnan's forests, Dianjunus rex weighs up to 2.2 kg, the biggest in its group of 64,000+ species. Its family line began about 65 million years ago.
🔗 Phys.org: https://phys.org/news/2026-10-scientists-d-rex-giant-fungus.html

✨ Fun fact: D-rex's family arose about 1 million years after the asteroid that ended the dinosaurs.
