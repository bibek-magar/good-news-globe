🦍 Lulingu was an 18-month-old orphan taken by traffickers. Today she's raising her own baby in Congo's Virunga forests + 3 more wins 👇

1. 🦍 A gorilla rescued from traffickers has her first wild baby
Lulingu was rescued at 18 months old and raised at the GRACE sanctuary. Released on Mount Tshiaberimu in 2024, she joined a wild silverback's group and gave birth in May.
🔗 The Economic Times: https://m.economictimes.com/magazines/panache/gorilla-rescued-as-an-orphan-from-traffickers-gives-birth-in-congos-virunga-national-park-why-baby-matters-for-the-endangered-primate/amp_articleshow/134661390.cms

2. 🪙 Norway's biggest Viking hoard just passed 5,000 coins
Detectorists found the Mørstad hoard in April with the landowner. The count has now reached about 5,200, and four coins of King Harald Hardrada date the burial to around 1046.
🔗 Arkeonews (@arkeo_news): https://arkeonews.net/norways-record-viking-hoard-has-passed-5000-coins-and-more-are-still-emerging/

3. 🐟 A tiny fish unseen in Sri Lanka since 1936 is back
Researchers found yellow-stripe gobies, just 14 to 19 mm long, in a tidal pool on Karaitivu Island near Jaffna, an area left unsurveyed for decades during the civil war.
🔗 The Morning: https://www.themorning.lk/articles/07zBB41kbe07wPPZttzo

4. 🌕 Moon soil from the far side holds a 'magnetic fossil'
Scientists studying Chang'e-6 samples found nanoscale γ-iron, a magnetic form of iron never before seen in natural lunar samples, holding a stable magnetic 'vortex'.
🔗 ScienceDaily: https://www.sciencedaily.com/releases/2026/10/261001214015.htm
