📸 Giant armadillos breed only once every 3 years. A camera trap in the Peruvian Amazon just filmed a baby. Follow for 3 good-news wins a day

1. 📸 A rare baby giant armadillo was filmed in the Amazon
A camera trap set with help from the Indigenous community of San Francisco caught a baby giant armadillo. Filming one can take over a year and hundreds of cameras.
🔗 People: https://people.com/rare-footage-shows-baby-giant-armadillo-in-the-peruvian-amazon-12190404

2. 🐟 Scientists found up to 2.8 million eels on one seamount
Over 9 sonar surveys, CSIRO found a huge gathering of basketwork eels at Patience Seamount in Huon Marine Park, the only one of about 120 nearby seamounts with it.
🔗 Phys.org: https://phys.org/news/2026-10-scientists-million-eels-underwater-mountain.html

3. 🦷 A painted-on liquid stopped tooth decay without a drill
In a trial of 830 children under 6, silver diamine fluoride painted on every 6 months stopped decay in more than half of their decayed baby teeth. No shots or drilling.
🔗 Good News Network: https://www.goodnewsnetwork.org/fda-approval-for-drill-free-cavity-care-may-be-imminent-after-great-phase-3-treatment-trial/

4. 💧 A plant-inspired filter will catch microplastics in sewage
PolyGone Systems, started by 2 Princeton graduates, copies how water-shedding plants trap particles. A 2-year pilot will run at the Atlantic County Utilities Authority.
🔗 Good News Network: https://www.goodnewsnetwork.org/nature-inspired-tech-prevents-microplastics-entering-atlantic-ocean-through-wastewater/
