📸 A rare baby giant armadillo was filmed in the Amazon

A camera trap set with help from the Indigenous community of San Francisco caught a baby giant armadillo. Filming one can take over a year and hundreds of cameras.

Why it's cool: The footage will back a proposed law to protect the species in Loreto.
