🏔️ Solar panels on an Alpine dam make 43% of their power in winter

4,872 panels hang on the Muttsee dam about 2,500 m up. In its first measured year the plant made 43% of its power in the winter half, 3.8 times a lowland plant's winter output.

Why it's cool: Mountain solar fills Switzerland's winter power gap, when it's needed most.
