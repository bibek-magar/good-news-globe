☀️🏔️ 4,872 solar panels hang on a Swiss dam 2,500 m up, and they make 43% of their power in winter + 3 more wins 👇

1. 🏔️ Solar panels on an Alpine dam make 43% of their power in winter
4,872 panels hang on the Muttsee dam about 2,500 m up. In its first measured year the plant made 43% of its power in the winter half, 3.8 times a lowland plant's winter output.
🔗 ECOnews: https://www.ecoticias.com/en/swiss-dam-solar-panels-winter-power/37035/

2. 💧 Somalia cut cholera deaths by 93%
The WHO's Eastern Mediterranean office says Somalia cut cholera deaths by 93% and re-established a national medicines regulator after more than 30 years.
🔗 Tanzania Times: https://tanzaniatimes.net/somalia-cuts-down-cholera-deaths-by-93-percent-as-sudan-increases-vaccination-coverage-to-more-than-71-percent/

3. 🏛️ Mohenjo-daro grew fairer as it grew bigger, homes reveal
Comparing house sizes at the Indus city, researchers found inequality fell over time as homes lined up with its planned streets, hinting that shared public works kept wealth gaps small.
🔗 HeritageDaily: https://www.heritagedaily.com/2026/10/study-reveals-declining-inequality-in-bronze-age-city-of-mohenjo-daro/159546

4. 🐱 Wyoming became the 6th US state where every shelter is no-kill
Every Wyoming shelter saved at least 90% of its pets for 12 straight months, 92% overall, Best Friends Animal Society says. A rescue taking in sick cats and kittens helped close the gap.
🔗 Best Friends Animal Society: https://bestfriends.org/who-we-are/media/wyoming-now-no-kill-state

✨ Fun fact: The Muttsee panels are tilted at 51 or 65 degrees so snow slides off them.
