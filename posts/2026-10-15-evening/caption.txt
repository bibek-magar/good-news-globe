🐢 Giant tortoises on Aldabra fell below 1,000. Now about 183,000 roam free, more than the Seychelles' people + 3 more wins 👇

1. 🐢 Hunted to under 1,000, giant tortoises now outnumber people
19th-century hunting left fewer than 1,000 tortoises on Aldabra. After decades of protection, a new count estimates about 183,000 grown tortoises, more than the Seychelles' whole human population.
🔗 PEOPLE: https://people.com/nearly-183000-giant-tortoises-roam-seychelles-aldabra-atoll-12164811

2. 🪵 Neanderthals carved a wooden club unlike anything ever found
A rock shelter near Barcelona kept the hollow shapes of Neanderthal wooden tools: spears up to 2.45 m long, bark trays and a club with no clear match in prehistory, from 62,000 to 40,000 years ago.
🔗 Earth.com: https://www.earth.com/science/neanderthal-wooden-club-abric-romani/

3. 🦗 11,000 of the world's heaviest insects are back in the wild
Wētāpunga, giant wētā as heavy as a mouse, once clung on to a single island. A 15-year breeding effort released 11,000 onto 8 predator-free islands and 2 sanctuaries, and they now breed there.
🔗 PEOPLE: https://uk.news.yahoo.com/11-000-world-heaviest-insects-200314229.html

4. 🪸 Bleached corals fully recovered after touching a healthy twin
Stanford researchers placed bleached coral against a healthy clone of itself. Their tissues fused, the healthy one shared its algae, and the bleached coral fully recovered in just 7 weeks.
🔗 SFGATE: https://www.yahoo.com/news/science/articles/stanford-discovers-way-save-ghostly-190000411.html
