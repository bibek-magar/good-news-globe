🌍 4 good things happening in the world right now.

1. A buried wall may finally reveal where Paris was born
Archaeologists digging in the courtyards of the Hôtel-Dieu hospital on the Île de la Cité uncovered a Gallic stone-and-wood wall dated to 200–10 BCE, alongside a spearhead and signs of fire.
Source: Good News Network (https://www.goodnewsnetwork.org/this-stone-wall-seems-to-have-finally-settled-debate-about-the-town-that-became-paris/)

2. Former hunters now get paid to photograph Borneo's wildlife
The KehatiKu app pays 800+ villagers in West Kalimantan for wildlife photos and recordings, with the biggest rewards for rare sightings like Bornean orangutans.
Source: Good News Network (https://www.goodnewsnetwork.org/a-strategy-of-cash-for-photos-turns-animal-hunters-into-animal-protectors-in-indonesia/)

3. Norway protects a 714-square-mile haven for whales and corals
The new Andfjorden Marine Protected Area in northern Norway shelters coral reefs, kelp forests and spawning grounds, plus large numbers of whales and seabirds.
Source: Good News Network (https://www.goodnewsnetwork.org/norways-newest-marine-protected-area-shelters-rich-marine-habitat-whales-and-seabirds/)

4. A 12-year-old's diaper drive delivers 30,000 diapers
Sloan started collecting diapers at age 7 after seeing empty shelves at Nourish Phoenix. Her fifth annual drive just brought in 30,000.
Source: Good News Network (https://www.goodnewsnetwork.org/12-yos-annual-diaper-drive-dumps-30000-diapers-off-at-arizona-food-bank/)

✨ Fun fact: Paris is named after the Parisii, the Celtic tribe who lived on the Île de la Cité over 2,000 years ago.
