🦏 A 50,000-year-old woolly rhino skull, teeth still intact, pulled from an English cave is now on show + 3 more wins 👇

1. 🦏 A 50,000-year-old woolly rhino skull is now on show
Dug out of Fairy Cave Quarry in Somerset in 2025 with other bones, the skull is now at the Wells and Mendip Museum. Experts think the rhino was about 40 when it fell into the crevice where it lay.
🔗 BBC News: https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/cm040z6712dro

2. 🧠 A new drug eased early Parkinson's signs in a small trial
30 people with a sleep disorder that often comes before Parkinson's took SNT-4728. Compared with 11 on a placebo, their movement improved, and the gain still held 12 weeks after they stopped.
🔗 Positive News: https://www.positive.news/society/good-news-stories-from-week-41-of-2026/

3. 🕳️ A giant black hole 'burped' jets about 300 days after a meal
Using NASA's Swift and radio telescopes, astronomers watched the black hole in galaxy NGC 1275 flare in X-rays, then found tentative signs its radio jets brightened roughly 300 days later.
🔗 Space.com: https://www.space.com/astronomy/black-holes/nasa-spacecraft-sees-supermassive-black-hole-gorge-on-a-cosmic-meal-before-burping-radio-jets-300-days-later

4. 🎬 A moviegoer raised $146,000 for a theater worker's retirement
Brooklyn Green, 21, saw an older woman with a bent back hauling trash at her cinema and started a GoFundMe. It raised over $100,000 in a day for Mary, who has worked in theaters for 45+ years.
🔗 Good News Network: https://www.goodnewsnetwork.org/elderly-theater-worker-surprised-with-146000-for-retirement-crowdfunded-by-young-moviegoer/
