🐧 Scientists named the 1st new penguin species in over 100 years, hiding among gentoo penguins + 3 more wins you missed 👇

1. 🐧 Gentoo penguins were secretly 4 species, and 1 is brand new
Reading the genomes of 64 gentoos from 10 colonies, scientists found 4 separate species. One, the southeastern gentoo of the remote Kerguelen Islands, is new to science.
🔗 ZME Science: https://www.zmescience.com/science/news-science/new-penguin-species-discovered-after-more-than-100-years/

2. 🐃 Wild buffalo are back in an Indian state after about 100 years
8 wild buffalo, 6 females and 2 males, travelled ~2,200 km from Kaziranga and now roam a 17-hectare enclosure in Kanha Tiger Reserve. The goal: 50 animals in 3 years.
🔗 ThePrint: https://theprint.in/india/wild-buffaloes-return-to-mp-after-century-state-targets-50-in-three-years/3068769/

3. 🌳 Volunteers will plant a 200 km ring of trees around London
The London Tree Ring invites schools and community groups to plant and care for a green corridor over 25 years. Pilots already planted 1,500 trees, including rare disease-resistant elm.
🔗 Positive News (@positivenewsuk): https://www.positive.news/society/putting-a-ring-on-it-the-plan-to-green-londons-beloved-edges/

4. 🪲 A teen lay in the dirt to win with a dung beetle photo
Oscar Whitfield shot a dung beetle rolling its ball at eye level, flat on the ground. It won Young Wildlife Photographer of the Year in a contest with a record 65,403 entries.
🔗 PEOPLE: https://people.com/wildlife-photographer-of-the-year-2026-winners-see-photos-12173016

✨ Fun fact: Before this, the last new penguin species was named in 1921: the northern rockhopper.
