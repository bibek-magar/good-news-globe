🦴 Fossils dug up in China in the 1970s turned out to be Denisovans, a lost human group, and now we know their face + 3 more wins 👇

1. 🦴 Fossils found in the 1970s turned out to be Denisovans
20 fossils from Xujiayao in northern China were a puzzle for decades. Ancient proteins now show they're Denisovans, an extinct human group with a sloping forehead and a huge brow ridge.
🔗 Nature: https://www.nature.com/articles/d41586-026-03360-0

2. ⛏️ A 9,000-year-old copper workshop was found in Türkiye
At Çayönü, archaeologists uncovered a 5 m² stone-paved work area where early farmers shaped green malachite and copper into beads. About 42 malachite and copper finds came out of it.
🔗 Türkiye Today: https://www.turkiyetoday.com/culture/archaeologists-in-turkiye-find-9000-year-old-copper-workshop-3230729

3. ☀️ Solar power saved Spain €17 billion in 18 months
From January 2025 to June 2026, solar cut Spain's average wholesale power price to €60 a MWh, versus about €112 without it, a study by the national solar association found.
🔗 Vozpópuli (@vozpopuli): https://www.vozpopuli.com/economia/energia/la-energia-solar-ha-ahorrado-mas-de-17000-millones-de-euros-a-los-consumidores-espanoles-en-solo-18-meses.html

4. 🦐 A giant sea bug in a museum for decades is a new species
Caught off Puerto Rico in the 1980s, this 32 cm giant isopod sat in a German collection under the wrong name. A CT scan revealed a comb of 15 bristles on its legs no relative has.
🔗 Nature World News: https://www.natureworldnews.com/articles/73601/20261021/puerto-ricos-newly-named-giant-isopod-points-15-million-year-journey-through-vanished-panama.htm

✨ Fun fact: The new isopod's name honours Boríken, the Indigenous Taíno name for Puerto Rico.
