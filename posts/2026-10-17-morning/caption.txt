🗿 A man working his land in Egypt found 2 giant statues of the pharaoh Ramesses II + 3 more wins you missed 👇

1. 🗿 A man working his land found 2 giant Ramesses II statues
Khaled el-Shahat found the statues in his village and called in experts. The bigger one once stood about 8.5 m tall; its head alone is 1.6 m and wears the double crown of Egypt.
🔗 Live Science (@live_science): https://www.livescience.com/archaeology/ancient-egyptians/2-giant-ramesses-ii-statues-one-wearing-a-double-crown-discovered-by-man-in-egypt

2. ⚛️ Physicists found signs of quantum 'strings' predicted in 1931
Hans Bethe worked out on paper that flipped spins in a row of particles can bind and travel together. A team chilled cesium atoms into thousands of tiny lines and saw evidence of it.
🔗 Live Science (via Yahoo) (@live_science): https://www.yahoo.com/news/science/articles/physicists-evidence-weird-quantum-strings-204506836.html

3. 🦞 A friendly brag led to North America's biggest crayfish
A biologist's giant 'Crawzilla' sparked a nationwide hunt through museum drawers. The winner: a Tennessee bottlebrush crayfish with an 86.6 mm body, about the length of a credit card.
🔗 ScienceDaily: https://www.sciencedaily.com/releases/2026/10/261015073220.htm

4. 🛺 An auto driver held a woman above water in a well for an hour
Pappachan Konoor was delivering a framed photo when a neighbour fell into a 16-foot well. He climbed in and kept her afloat for nearly an hour until firefighters pulled them out.
🔗 Onmanorama (@onmanorama): https://www.onmanorama.com/news/kerala/2026/10/16/autorickshaw-driver-saves-woman-from-well-in-kasaragod.html
