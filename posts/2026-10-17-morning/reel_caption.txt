🗿 A man working his land found 2 giant Ramesses II statues

Khaled el-Shahat found the statues in his village and called in experts. The bigger one once stood about 8.5 m tall; its head alone is 1.6 m and wears the double crown of Egypt.

Why it's cool: Ramesses II ruled for 66 years, one of the longest reigns in Egypt's history.
