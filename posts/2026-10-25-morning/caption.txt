🌌 Astronomers matched van Gogh's Starry Night to a real dawn sky from July 1889 + 3 more wins you missed 👇

1. 🌌 Van Gogh's Starry Night matches a real dawn sky from 1889
A South Korean team found the painting's moon, Venus and Hyades star cluster line up with the sky over Saint-Rémy around July 22, 1889, a month later than its usual date.
🔗 AP News: https://apnews.com/article/van-gogh-starry-night-space-6385c8e37f748bddc4137cdda5c76887

2. 🌸 A flowering shrub last collected in 1816 was found again
Botanists from Gauhati University surveying Assam found Ardisia colorata, a shrub first collected in India around 1816 and described in 1821, after more than 2 centuries.
🔗 The Times of India: https://timesofindia.indiatimes.com/city/guwahati/rare-flowering-shrub-rediscovered-in-state-after-two-centuries/articleshow/135153168.cms

3. 🐻 A man saved a 4.4 lb bear cub. Now she's 330 lb and his best friend
Petar Tubic found Masa abandoned in a forest in 2019 and fed her milk and fruit. She still tries to sit on his lap, and he now also cares for wolves, deer and raccoons.
🔗 AP via San Bernardino Sun: https://www.sbsun.com/2026/10/23/bosnia-pet-bear/

4. 🚌 He leapt onto a runaway school bus and stopped it
Ebro Ahmed, 27, saw a bus roll by with its doors open and the driver having a seizure. He jumped aboard, pulled the parking brake, then stayed with the driver until paramedics came.
🔗 Good News Network (@goodnewsnetwork): https://www.goodnewsnetwork.org/st-paul-man-jumps-onto-runaway-school-bus-saves-18-children/

✨ Fun fact: You can find the Hyades yourself: a V of stars beside the reddish star Aldebaran in Taurus.
