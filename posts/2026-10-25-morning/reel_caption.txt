🌌 Van Gogh's Starry Night matches a real dawn sky from 1889

A South Korean team found the painting's moon, Venus and Hyades star cluster line up with the sky over Saint-Rémy around July 22, 1889, a month later than its usual date.

Why it's cool: Experts say it's likely a blend of several nights, but it shows van Gogh watched the real sky closely.
